## Summary
A trade review of a Bitcoin long that puts the earlier episodes together into a
top-down process. The order is: (1) higher-timeframe candle shape and narrative,
(2) market profile (highs and lows), (3) entry model. A monthly CRT drove a "flash
crash" into an old low with an FVG under it, so a bullish reaction was expected.
The entry was in the Tuesday London session on the 5-minute chart, after a
BTC/ETH SMT and a true MSS. The stop went at the turtle-soup low and the target was
the previous day's high. Promo for his paid mentorship (deadline 31 October 2025)
and motivational talk are skipped.

## Definitions
- **Model #1 (bearish)**: the **one specific candle** (not a zone of candles) that
  liquidated the old high. When price closes below it, a dump follows soon,
  usually after a retrace into that candle. Bullish version: the down-close candle
  that swept the old low, and price closes above it. *(1:51 to 2:43)*
- **Model #1 off a model #1**: a model #1 nested inside the candle of another model
  #1. *(2:43)*
- **True MSS (bullish)**: price stabs into an old low, then **closes** above "the
  high that broke the low", meaning the swing high formed just before the low was
  taken. Sequence: low, high, lower low, close above that high. *(8:46, 15:28)*
- **True MSS retrace zone**: the area between the high that broke the low and the
  turtle-soup low. This is the buy area. *(16:16)*
- **Price cycle**: range, turtle soup, trend, repeated. *(3:26)*
- **SMT = expansion**: a valid SMT is expected to lead to an expansion move. *(12:43, 16:56)*
- **Market profile**: reading the sequence of highs and lows (which are swept,
  which are broken). *(8:46)*
- ICT concepts used: **turtle soup**, **FVG**, **liquidity pool**, **SMT divergence**,
  **MSS**, **OTE**, **London killzone/session**, **daily bias / HTF narrative**,
  **PO3** (daily-candle framing).

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] When price hits a major target (an old liquidity pool), especially one with an
      FVG under it or near all-time highs, expect at least a reaction or retrace.
      Do not expect the trend to continue in a straight line. *(1:07, 4:11)*
- [ ] Process order before any trade: HTF candle shape/narrative, then market
      profile (highs/lows), then the entry model last. *(8:02, 9:30)*
- [ ] Take a trade only when confluences are "overwhelmingly one-sided". *(9:30)*
- [ ] Look for the entry within a specific time window, chosen from the HTF candle
      shape and market profile. *(11:14)*
- [ ] The trade is framed as catching a portion of **one daily candle** (Tuesday
      midnight to Wednesday midnight). *(10:07)*
- [ ] SMT: you must only take bullish SMTs when the higher timeframe is bullish,
      and bearish SMTs when the higher timeframe is bearish. *(12:43, 13:39)*
- [ ] SMT example: ETH swept a low while BTC did not and rallied. Expect price not
      to return to that low. *(12:15)*
- [ ] Every entry must be either a **model #1** or a **true MSS**. *(14:16, 14:46)*
- [ ] After the true MSS (close above the high that broke the low), wait for a
      retrace into the area between that high and the turtle-soup low (this
      "unlocks the OTE"), then buy. *(15:28, 16:16)*
- [ ] A weekly candle closing below the up-close candle that swept the old high is a
      partial model #1 (not all criteria met). Even if bearish, expect a pump back
      into that candle first. *(4:41, 5:50)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: on the 5-minute chart, in the retrace zone between the high that broke the
  low and the turtle-soup low, after the true MSS. Tuesday London session. *(15:28, 16:16)*
- Stop: the turtle-soup low. If the stop is the turtle-soup extreme you are unlikely
  to be swept. *(16:16)*
- Target: the previous day's high. He did not expect more because the turtle soup
  of the old high was "not very convincing" (not a hard stab). *(16:56 to 17:48)*
- Exit: either by **time** or by **price**; both are valid. *(18:14)*
- Timeframe pairs used: monthly CRT (narrative), weekly candle, daily candle (the
  trade), 5-minute (entry). *(0:00, 7:23, 10:07, 15:28)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: Tuesday, London session. *(10:07, 15:28)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **"HTF candle shape"**: not defined. Proposal: classify the last closed
  weekly/daily candle by body/wick ratio and by whether it swept a prior high/low
  and closed back inside (a turtle-soup candle) or closed beyond it.
- **"Overwhelmingly one-sided" confluences**: proposal: a score where HTF narrative,
  market profile, SMT and entry model must all agree (4/4).
- **"Not very convincing" turtle soup**: proposal: sweep depth < 0.25× ATR(14) on
  that TF counts as weak, so target only the nearest liquidity (previous day's high).
- **Time window for the entry**: he says to study his entry time as homework.
  Proposal: London killzone 02:00–05:00 New York time, on Tuesday/Wednesday.
- **Exit by time**: not specified. Proposal: close at the end of the session/killzone
  if the target is not hit.
- "Too many smooth edges" (equal highs/lows left behind as liquidity) is not
  defined. Proposal: ≥ 2 swing points within 0.1×ATR of each other.

## Conflicts with other sources
- Ep.7 says candle 3 entries use the OTE without needing to reach 50%. Here the entry
  zone is the whole range from the high that broke the low to the turtle-soup low,
  with no Fib level mentioned. These are compatible but not identical.
- Ep.7 says candle 1 has a model #1 and candle 3 an OTE. Here "every entry" is either
  a model #1 or a true MSS, which is a slightly different split.
