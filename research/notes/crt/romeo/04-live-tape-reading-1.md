## Summary
This is a ~43-minute live narration of one bearish 4H CRT trade, watched on a low
timeframe (1m mentioned, 1H checked). Romeo is short during candle 3 of a 4H CRT,
with the draw on liquidity at an old low. Price grinds down through several failed
bullish signals (model #1, MSS, "OTE") and reaches the target. The key new ideas:
the draw on liquidity outranks every opposing pattern, there is always a "kiss of
death" turtle soup before the target, SMT is the only exception, and a close above
the key high warns that the idea is failing. Much of the video is silence/music and
commentary on "pattern traders"; that is skipped.

## Definitions
- **4H CRT, bearish** *(0:00–0:15)*: C1, then C2 = turtle soup (sweep of C1 high),
  then C3 = distribution. C3 shape: "open, high, dump, and close".
- **Draw on liquidity (DOL)** *(0:15–1:40)*: the pending target, here an old low
  (marked with an "eye" icon). "The draw liquidity is paramount. It comes first."
  ICT concept: **draw on liquidity**.
- **Kiss of death turtle soup** *(0:32–1:08, 7:33–7:49, 15:24)*: a fake turtle soup
  (fake bottom for a bearish target, fake top for a bullish one) that forms just
  before price reaches the DOL. It traps counter-trend traders. He says it is
  "always" there before the target.
- **Mid-move consolidation** *(4:28–5:18, 12:10–12:43)*: price never reaches the
  target in one go. It grinds and chops in small ranges ("creating hope") and then
  dumps into the DOL.
- **Order flow (bearish)** *(3:18–4:08)*: old highs are briefly taken ("pop above")
  to stop out sellers and attract buyers, then price dumps. ICT concept: **bearish
  order flow / liquidity runs**.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Until the pending bearish DOL is reached, you must not take or consider any
      bullish pattern. The mirror holds for a bullish DOL *(1:40–1:56, 3:01–3:18)*.
- [ ] While bias is clearly bearish, keep selling until the DOL is reached
      *(10:11)*.
- [ ] Exception: **SMT divergence** is the only reason a target fails *(10:11–10:27)*.
      Example: an SMT between GBP, EUR and the dollar (DXY) *(15:40)*.
- [ ] An SMT counts only with confirmation: price closing **decisively, multiple
      times** above the key high. Only then reassess for buys *(16:04–16:20)*.
- [ ] Invalidation warning: if price **closes above the key high** (the high of the
      kiss-of-death TS / the high that started the move), that is a warning sign
      *(15:24–15:40, 17:09–17:48)*. Price should usually not trade above the kiss-
      of-death TS high. That is the "litmus test".
- [ ] Do not "marry your bias". Measure it against that high *(17:31–17:48)*.
- [ ] Model #1 against the bias may give a reaction, but it is counter-trend. Do not
      trade it *(29:31–30:24)*.
- [ ] In a ranging market, do not anticipate a trending move, and vice versa
      *(38:14–38:44)*.
- [ ] Failed signals listed against a valid HTF bias: failed model #1 (2:45),
      failed MSS "low, high, lower low, higher high" (6:05), fake OTE (18:33).
- [ ] When the LTF looks noisy, zoom out to the 4H CRT (C1, C2, C3) *(22:45–23:01)*.

- Time-of-day / session filters: none stated. He notes the time the high forms as

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **Which old low is the DOL**. Proposal: the nearest unswept HTF (4H or higher)
  swing low beyond C1's 50%, or C1's low itself (link to note 02's target).
- **Identifying the kiss-of-death TS in real time**. It is only clear afterwards.
  Proposal: the last LTF sweep of a swing low that is followed by a lower high,
  within X% of the distance to the DOL. Use it as a stop/invalidation anchor, not as
  an entry signal.
- **"Decisive" closes / "multiple times"**. Proposal: ≥2 LTF candle closes above
  the key high, each with a body ≥ 50% of its range.
- **SMT definition**. Proposal: within the same window, GBPUSD makes a lower low
  while EURUSD does not (or the reverse), with DXY confirming the inverse.
- **"Entering at the extremities"**. Proposal: sell only in the upper 25% of the
  current LTF range, or in premium of the most recent swing leg.
- **Rejection block** (31:14): ICT concept named but not used for a rule.

## Conflicts with other sources
- **Target**: here the target is an old low (DOL), with a partial "at the initial
  target". Note 02 says the target is 50% of C1, then the opposite extreme.
  These may coincide but are not the same rule.
- Note 02 says C3 is "usually the fastest" move. Here C3 is a slow grind with many
  consolidations. Not a strict contradiction, but it weakens a "fast C3" filter.
