## Summary
This episode covers which CRT candle to trade and the fixed lower-timeframe sequence
("journey") price follows from the turtle soup to the CRT target. Romeo tells
almost everyone to trade only candle 3. Candle 2 is only to be watched ("tape read").
In a bullish CRT, he says the LTF always shows a turtle soup at the low, a true market
structure shift (MSS), a model #1, a breaker, an OTE, and a kiss of death turtle soup before
the CRT high. The example is a weekly DXY / dollar CRT on the 4H that he says he called live. Ends with a
book-launch plug (2 Oct 2025).

## Definitions
- **Candle 1 / 2 / 3**: candle 1 = accumulation (sets range). Candle 2 = continues
  candle 1's direction, makes the manipulation / turtle soup and the reversal "where time
  meets price". Candle 3 onward = distribution toward the target (ICT **PO3 / AMD**). *(2:14–3:05, 7:34–8:08)*
- **Two-timeframe analysis**: HTF candle shape gives the bias. LTF "anatomy" gives the entry. *(3:21–4:27)*
- **Targets**: price moves only to (a) take a high/low (liquidity pool) or (b) fill an
  imbalance (FVG). A HTF gap is an LTF high/low, so "it's all in the highs and lows". *(8:43–9:17)*
- **The journey (bullish CRT, on the LTF)**: turtle soup of the CRT low, then model #1,
  breaker, OTE, kiss of death turtle soup, then CRT high. The legs are: target 50%, then old highs, then CRT high. *(8:08–8:26, 10:20–10:36, 13:00)*
- **Kiss of death turtle soup**: "the last turtle soup before a target is met". The target can be
  the CRT target or another one. *(8:26)*
- **True market structure shift (MSS)**: low, then high, then lower low, then a break above "the high
  that broke the low". "Breaker" is used as the same thing. Every CRT has one. It is
  marked by the TS and model #1. *(11:52–12:28, 17:39–18:00)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Trade candle 3 (the third HTF candle onward) only. Candle 2 is for advanced students only,
      "with extreme precaution". *(1:10–2:14, 5:50–6:59)*
- [ ] Bias for the whole candle-3 phase: buy until the CRT high is reached (bullish CRT). *(15:56–16:14)*
- [ ] Before entering on the LTF, you must see: a TS at the CRT low, a true MSS, a model #1. A
      KoD TS is expected before the CRT high. *(16:14–16:50)*
- [ ] True MSS rule: after the TS low, price must close/break above the swing high
      that preceded the lower low. *(17:39–18:00)*
- [ ] Expect slow, "lethargic" price near the extreme being formed (accumulation).
      Expect a fast move after the OTE or KoD. *(9:33–10:20)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: preferred entries are the turtle soup and model #1 (his "two favorite"). Then the
  breaker/OTE and KoD TS as re-entries. *(9:17)*
- Stop: not stated.
- Target: 50% of candle-1 range, then old highs, then CRT high. *(13:00)*
- Timeframe pairs used: Weekly CRT, then 4H anatomy. *(13:49, 16:50)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: "time" is said to be covered in later episodes. The weekly
  candles are marked from Sunday open. *(12:28, 18:51–19:07)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **"Where time meets price"**: time is not defined yet. Leave it as a placeholder.
- **Breaker vs MSS**: he uses the terms interchangeably. Implement a single "true MSS"
  event: after the swept low L1, take the last swing high H that formed before the lower low.
  An LTF close above H = MSS. Swing = N-bar fractal (N to calibrate, start at 2).
- **"Those are all certainties"**: this is a claim, not a rule. Test it statistically: how often
  each event appears inside successful weekly CRTs.
- **Candle 2 vs 3 boundary**: if candle 2 itself already starts distribution, it is not
  clear whether a candle-3 entry is still valid. Propose: allow entries only after the
  close of candle 2 and only if the target is still untouched.

## Conflicts with other sources
- ep.6 says manipulation can occur at the end of the previous candle, with distribution

  may show only minimal manipulation, not the full journey described here.
- ep.2 says the KoD is ideally in the lower 25% of the range. Here, the KoD coincided with the OTE.
  Both videos say it can be the OTE.
