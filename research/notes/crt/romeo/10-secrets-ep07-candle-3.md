## Summary
Episode 7 explains how to trade the third candle of a three-candle CRT, which
Romeo says beginners should trade instead of candle 2. Candle 1 is accumulation
just before a key level, candle 2 is manipulation (it sweeps the key level), and
candle 3 is distribution (the fast expansion). You wait for candle 2 to close,
then look for a turtle soup around candle 3's open. Targets are the 50% of the
range first, then the opposite end of candle 1. He frames every trade as
"trading one candle" (monthly, weekly, daily, 4H, or 1H at most) inside a
higher-timeframe candle. He opens with roughly 2.5 minutes of complaining about
plagiarists and closes with motivational talk.

Recorded around September 2025 (he refers to "October the 2nd" as an upcoming date).

## Definitions
- **Key level**: a prior swing high/low (example: "this old low is the key level")
  that candle 2 must sweep. Trading CRT without a key level is "a beginner
  mistake". *(2:27, 3:54)* How to pick the *correct* key level is not taught (see ep.10).
- **Candle 1 (accumulation)**: the candle right before price reaches the key level.
  In a bullish setup it is usually a "thick down close candle right before the key
  level". *(3:01, 4:09)*
- **Candle 2 (manipulation)**: the candle that runs through the key level (the stop
  hunt). Slow, choppy, "lethargic". Beginners should not trade it. *(4:26, 7:48)*
- **Candle 3 (distribution)**: the fast expansion candle after candle 2 closes. *(3:17, 7:31)*
- **CRT levels**: CRT low, CRT 50%, CRT high, drawn on the range being traded. *(5:50)*
  From context, the range is candle 1's high-low.
- **Kiss of death turtle soup**: the last turtle soup (a sweep of a high/low that
  then reverses) of a key level, which "always" comes before price reaches the CRT
  target. It is sometimes, but not always, the same thing as the OTE. *(14:29, 17:16, 18:54)*
- **Two-candle CRT**: when candle 2 itself expands quickly toward the target, so
  there is no candle-3 entry. Named but not defined further. *(23:06)*
- **"Candle trader"**: before every trade, name which candle you are trading
  (for example "the daily CRT within the weekly candle"). *(11:44 to 12:31)*
- ICT concepts used: **turtle soup** (liquidity sweep and reversal), **OTE**
  (Fibonacci retracement entry), **MSS** ("true market structure shift"), **AMD/PO3**
  (accumulation-manipulation-distribution mapped onto candles 1/2/3), **liquidity**.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] A CRT must always be paired with a key level. *(2:27, 3:33)*
- [ ] Beginners must not trade candle 2. Trade candle 3 only. *(4:43)*
- [ ] Wait for candle 2 to **close** before trading. A candle 2 that looks bearish
      mid-candle can keep printing higher and close above, so the shape is only
      known at the close. *(6:08 to 6:56)*
- [ ] After candle 3 opens, wait for a turtle soup: in a bearish case price pops
      above a high, then reverses down. Invert for bullish. *(7:12)*
- [ ] General rule: candle 1 contains a "model #1"; candle 3 contains an OTE. *(8:55)*
- [ ] Candle 3 usually either continues the true MSS formed inside it, or makes the
      true break of structure and continues. *(9:12)*
- [ ] Every trade is inside one candle: monthly, weekly, daily, or at most 4H.
      1H is allowed only for "hyper scalpers". *(11:11 to 11:44)*
- [ ] Use the weekly open (Sunday) and weekly close (Friday) as the framework for a
      daily CRT. *(10:54, 12:15)*
- [ ] A higher-timeframe CRT target that is still pending must override
      lower-timeframe bullish structure (for example a convincing bullish MSS
      against a pending HTF target below can be faded). *(13:55, 18:06)*
- [ ] OTE: draw the Fib from the swing high to the low. Price does **not** have to
      reach the 50%. In a strong trend the retrace stops at a shallower fib level.
      If price nearly reaches 50% and turtle-soups a key level, take the entry
      anyway. *(19:11 to 21:44)*
- [ ] If candle 3 never gives the entry you wanted, skip the trade (no chasing). *(23:26)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: after candle 2 closes, in candle 3, on the far side of candle 3's open
  (bullish: "trade below the open"), on a turtle soup / OTE retracement. *(24:16)*
- Stop: not stated in this video (ep.9 suggests the turtle-soup extreme).
- Target: CRT 50% first, then the opposite end of candle 1 (bullish: candle 1 high;
  bearish: candle 1 low), then check for further targets. *(12:31, 24:33)*
- Timeframe pairs used: daily CRT inside the weekly candle (EUR example). *(12:15)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: none given. Monday/Tuesday/Wednesday mentioned in
  passing only. *(9:46)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **"Thick" down-close candle 1**: propose body ≥ 1.5× the median body of the last
  20 candles on that TF, and close in the bottom 30% of its range.
- **Key level**: not defined. Proposal: the nearest unswept fractal swing high/low
  (5-bar pivot) on the same or higher TF, within N ATR of candle 1's close.
- **"Model #1 inside candle 1"**: depends on the model #1 definition (see ep.8/ep.9).
- **Turtle soup in candle 3**: proposal: on the LTF (for example M15 for a daily
  CRT), price trades beyond the most recent swing high (bearish) after candle 3's
  open, then an LTF candle closes back below that swing.
- **"Almost reaches the 50%"**: proposal: retrace reaches ≥ 0.382 of the leg.
- **"Lured in enough liquidity"**: not measurable as stated. Drop it or use a sweep
  of at least one LTF swing.
- **Kiss of death**: proposal: the last sweep of a swing point (on the entry TF)
  that happens inside candle 3 before price reaches the CRT 50%.

## Conflicts with other sources
- At 19:30 he says expecting the 50% to always hit in an OTE "is a requirement",
  then argues the opposite (price often does not reach 50%). This is most likely a
  slip for "is a mistake". Treat the 50% retrace as optional.
- Ep.8 says the CRT target is always the 50% first. This episode agrees, and adds
  the opposite end of candle 1 as the second target.
