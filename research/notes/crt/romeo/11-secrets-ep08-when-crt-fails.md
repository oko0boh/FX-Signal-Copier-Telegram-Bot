## Summary
Romeo lists three reasons a CRT "fails" (he argues the system never fails, only the
trader). (1) An SMT against the trade appears on the way to the target. (2) Price
has already reached the 50% of the range, which counts as a win, so a reversal
after it is not a failure. (3) The trader picked a CRT against the higher-timeframe
CRT direction. Reasons 1 and 2 are explained with Bitcoin examples; reason 3 is
left as homework. About 2.5 minutes at the start promote his paid mentorship
(sign-ups closing 21 October 2025).

## Definitions
- **Candle SMT**: compare the **same candle** on two correlated assets (Bitcoin vs
  Ethereum). An SMT exists when that candle turtle-soups (sweeps) a prior low on
  one asset and the same candle on the other asset does not. *(7:41 to 8:13)*
- **SMT confirmation**: confirm the SMT with either a model #1 or a true MSS
  targeting the opposite direction. *(8:13, 9:47)*
- **Model #1 (as used here)**: the one specific down-close candle that swept
  (liquidated) the old low. A close back above it confirms the bullish reversal.
  He calls this candle "the most consistent catalyst of large price moves". *(8:30 to 9:02)*
- **True MSS (bullish)**: low, high, lower low, then higher high (a close above the
  high that formed before the lower low). Followed by an OTE retrace, which he
  describes as a "second equal leg". *(9:17 to 9:32)*
- **CRT target #1**: always 50% of the candle range. Reaching it means the CRT
  worked. *(12:17, 12:48)*
- **CRT complete**: price reaches the opposite end of the candle range. *(13:20)*
- ICT concepts used: **SMT divergence**, **turtle soup / liquidity sweep**, **MSS**,
  **OTE**, **HTF bias**.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Failure case 1 (SMT): if price is heading to a CRT target and, before it gets
      there, a CRT in the opposite direction forms **with an SMT and a
      confirmation**, drop the original target and follow the new direction. "SMT
      stops targets from being met." *(6:19, 10:38, 16:07)*
- [ ] SMT check: one specific candle on asset A sweeps the old low; the same candle
      on correlated asset B does not. *(8:13)*
- [ ] SMT must be confirmed by a close above the down-close candle that swept the
      old low (model #1), or by a true MSS. *(8:30, 9:47)*
- [ ] Failure case 2 (50%): a CRT that reaches the 50% and then reverses is a
      **valid, winning** CRT. After reaching 50%, watch price to decide whether it
      continues to the opposite end. This applies to entries in candle 1, 2 or 3. *(12:17 to 13:36)*
- [ ] Trading only from the CRT high/low to the 50% is a complete, valid strategy
      on its own. *(13:04)*
- [ ] Failure case 3 (wrong selection): you must not take bearish lower-timeframe
      CRTs inside a higher-timeframe bullish CRT (on the way from point A to the 50%
      and target), and vice versa. Counter-trend CRTs sometimes work, but with-trend
      CRTs are more consistent. *(13:51 to 16:56)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: not detailed. Implied: after a confirmed SMT, enter in the direction of the
  new CRT (BTC example: buy the OTE after the true MSS). *(9:17)*
- Stop: not stated.
- Target: 50% of the CRT range first (mandatory first target), then the opposite
  end. *(12:17)*
- Timeframe pairs used: higher-timeframe CRT gives direction; lower-timeframe CRTs
  must align. Specific TFs not named, except "let's go to the daily" for the 50% example. *(11:45, 16:41)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: none.

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **Correlated pair choice**: only BTC/ETH shown. Proposal: use a fixed list
  (EURUSD/GBPUSD vs DXY inverted, ES/NQ, BTC/ETH).
- **"Turtle souped" on the SMT candle**: proposal: candle low < prior swing low
  (fractal) and candle close > that swing low. The other asset's same-time candle
  low stays > its corresponding swing low.
- **Which old low**: proposal: the most recent fractal swing low on the same TF as
  the candle used.
- **"Point A / point B"** for the HTF direction: proposal: point A = the HTF CRT
  sweep extreme, point B = the HTF CRT target (opposite end of the range). LTF CRTs
  are allowed only in the A→B direction.
- **"Monitor how price acts" after 50%**: no rule given. Proposal: take partial
  profit at 50%; hold the rest only if no opposite-direction LTF MSS forms.

## Conflicts with other sources
