## Summary
This is the core definition lecture for Candle Range Theory. Every candle on every
timeframe is a range, and the next candle either breaks out of it (closes beyond) or
turtle-soups it (wicks beyond, then closes back inside). The setup is a three-candle
sequence: candle 1 = range (accumulation), candle 2 = sweep of one side
(manipulation), candle 3 = expansion to the range's 50% and then the opposite
extreme (distribution). That is ICT **Power of Three / AMD** mapped onto specific
candles. The lecture also gives a next-candle bias rule from closes vs wicks, a lower-
timeframe entry ("model #1"), and an entry sequence (TS, then order block, then
breaker/OTE). Promo talk (claimed "80% accuracy world record", criticism of ICT)
is skipped.

## Definitions
- **CRT (Candle Range Theory)**: "every single candle is a range" *(1:31–1:47)*.
  The range is the candle's high to low, with open and close marked.
- **Two outcomes only** for the candle after a reference candle *(2:04–2:38)*:
  - *Breakout*: the next candle **closes** beyond the reference high/low and
    continues.
  - *Turtle soup*: the next candle **wicks** beyond the high/low and **closes back
    inside** the range. Then expect a move the other way.
  - Inside bars may come in between. "Candle 1, inside bars, then turtle soup" is
    treated as the same result *(2:38)*.
- **CRT high / CRT low**: the high and low of the reference candle (candle 1)
  *(16:32, 34:37–34:54)*.
- **Candle 1 / 2 / 3** *(17:49–18:38, 26:54–28:17, 40:14)*:
  - C1 = the reference range ("accumulation");
  - C2 = sweeps one side of C1 ("manipulation"). Romeo advises avoiding trades in C2
    if you can (27:11);
  - C3 = "distribution" to the target. It is usually the fastest move *(28:17, 41:03)*.
- **Next-candle bias rule** *(35:09–35:57)*:
  - close above previous candle's high → higher prices next candle;
  - close below previous candle's low → lower prices next candle;
  - wick above previous high (close back inside) → lower prices next candle;
  - wick below previous low (close back inside) → higher prices next candle.
  ICT concept: **daily bias** from the previous candle.
- **Key level**: used together with "liquidity pool" and "old high / old low" (11:04).
  A key level on the TF **higher** than the one whose PO3 you are trading
  (10:47–11:04). The high may be a wick or a body (13:32–13:49).
- **Model #1 (entry)** *(37:21–39:22)*: on the lower TF, find the one candle that
  stabbed the old low (bullish). It should be a thick **down-close** candle. Wait for
  a later candle to **close above that candle**. Bearish is the mirror: a thick
  up-close candle that stabbed an old high, then a close below it. "Ignore the wicks
  look at the bodies" *(38:15)*. ICT concept: closest to an **order block** / turtle-
  soup candle, though Romeo says "it's not supply and demand" (39:06).
- **Failure swing**: after the TS low forms, price drops back almost to that low once
  more ("scare everyone") before rallying *(33:29–34:01)*.
- **Range → trend**: markets alternate range and trend. The first breakout of a range
  is usually fake (a TS), then the trend starts *(30:13–31:18)*.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Step 1: pick the range (candle TF) to trade: monthly, weekly, daily or 4H
      *(4:35, 8:33–9:06)*. Romeo mainly trades daily and 4H, and weekly "if clean".
- [ ] Bearish template: "open → key level → turtle soup → decline". Bullish:
      "open → bullish key level / old low → TS → rally" *(10:31–11:19, 13:17)*.
- [ ] After C2 turtle-soups one side of C1, price will "with an extremely high
      probability" trade **at least to C1's 50%** *(16:48–17:03)*.
- [ ] The trigger may be "any candle turtle soup **or SMT**" and then 50% *(17:03)*
      (ICT **SMT divergence**).
- [ ] In C3, bearish: sell **above the open** of C3. Bullish: buy **below the open**
      of C3. He mis-says "sell below" at 22:32. All OBs, breakers and TS form above
      the open for bearish and below it for bullish *(21:11–22:48)*. ICT concept:
      **PO3 / Judas swing** relative to the open.
- [ ] Weekly range: C1 = weekly candle from Sunday 17:00 NY to the next Sunday 17:00
      *(17:34)*. "True week open of course is Monday" (midnight) *(17:49)*. For a
      bearish weekly, sell above the **Monday 00:00 NY open** while watching price
      from Sunday 17:00 *(21:44–22:17)*.
- [ ] Daily range resets at **midnight New York** *(9:57)*. All times are New York
      time "because that's the time the algorithm functions" *(22:00)*.
- [ ] Monthly AMD by weeks: week 1 accumulation, week 2 manipulation, week 3+
      distribution *(16:17)*.
- [ ] Never buy above a high or sell below a low *(28:17)*.
- [ ] Do not force a TS. If C2 **closes** beyond the C1 extreme, it is a breakout:
      "let it go" and wait for another *(42:55–43:11)*.
- [ ] Entry sequence in time order *(39:38–40:14)*: entry 1 = TS (model #1);
      entry 2 = **order block**; entry 3 = **market structure shift**, then a
      **breaker** or **OTE** for the second leg. If the breaker or OTE has already
      been hit, do not look for entry 1.
- [ ] Lows at the 50% of any price leg usually get turtle-souped *(20:21)*.
- [ ] Every candle's high and low form at specific times. Time beats price
      *(42:25–42:40)*. The details are not given here.

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry options:
  (a) most advanced: inside C2 (at the sweep) *(37:21)*;
  (b) model #1 on the LTF: on the close above the thick down-close candle that
      stabbed the low *(37:36–38:49)*;
  (c) simplest: C3, selling above C3's open for bearish or buying below it for
      bullish *(21:11–21:44)*.
- Stop: not stated explicitly. Implied beyond the swept extreme / the model #1
  candle (see note 03).
- Target: **50% of C1** (partial "always" at 50%), then the **opposite extreme** of
  C1 *(18:21–18:38, 28:00–28:17)*. He explicitly rejects using the nearest FVG
  (ICT **IRL→ERL**) as the target; the 50% is "the correct technical target"
  *(29:23–29:40)*.
- Timeframe pairs used (for viewing a candle's PO3 on the LTF) *(5:30–6:23)*:
  Monthly→4H, Weekly→1H, Daily→15m, 4H→5m. Romeo says no TF alignment is fixed
  *(7:46–8:17)*.
- Time-of-day / session filters: daily open 00:00 NY. Weekly: watch from Sunday
  17:00 NY and trade around the Monday 00:00 NY open. No killzone named.

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **"Thick" down/up-close candle**. Proposal: body ≥ 60% of the candle range and
  range ≥ 1.0× ATR(14) on that TF. A "flimsy" candle fails this.
- **"Close above this candle"**: above its body high or its wick high? He says
  "ignore the wicks look at the bodies" but also "from the high to the wick".
  Proposal: trigger = LTF close > high of the model-#1 candle (wick high). Test the
  body high as a variant.
- **Key level**: not defined beyond "old high/low / liquidity pool on the HTF".
  Proposal: unswept fractal swing highs/lows on the TF above the range TF, plus prior
  candle highs/lows (PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL).
- **Choosing the bias / direction** before C2 completes. Proposal: apply the next-
  candle rule (35:09) to the candle before C1, plus HTF premium/discount.
- **"Clean" weekly range**: undefined. Proposal: weekly range ≥ X×ATR(weekly) and C2
  has not closed beyond C1.
- **Failure swing**: "almost" retests the low. Proposal: a retrace to within 25% of
  the C2 sweep extension, without a new low, within N LTF bars.
- **Time of high/low formation**: mentioned but no rules here (see note 03).

## Conflicts with other sources
- **Weekly open**: here, weekly C1 runs Sunday 17:00 → Sunday 17:00 NY, while the
  "true" open is Monday 00:00 *(17:34–17:49)*. Note 03 uses Monday 00:00 open and
  Friday 17:00 close.
- **TF pairs**: here M→4H, W→1H, D→15m, 4H→5m (for viewing PO3). Note 03 uses
  M→D, W→4H, D→1H, 4H→15m (for model #1). They may serve different purposes, but
  they are inconsistent.
- **Target**: here 50% of C1 then the opposite extreme. Note 03 says "target the
  highs/lows one by one". Note 04 uses the draw on liquidity with a partial at the
  initial target.
