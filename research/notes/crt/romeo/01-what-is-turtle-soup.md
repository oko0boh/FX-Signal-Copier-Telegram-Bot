## Summary
Episode 1 of Romeo's six-part public "Turtle Soup manual". It defines turtle soup
(TS) as a false breakout or false breakdown of an old high or old low. It explains
the liquidity logic behind it: stops of earlier traders plus breakout orders rest
beyond the level, and price is pushed through to fill those orders before it
reverses. The episode only lists the trading rules. Entry models, market profiles,
timing and stop placement are left for later episodes (incl. a "million dollar FVG
model", 1:38). Roughly the first 5 minutes are study advice (unlearn other methods,
one mentor only) and are skipped here.

## Definitions
- **Turtle soup (TS)**: "a false breakout or a false breakdown" *(6:45)*. From the
  market maker's view: deliberately pushing price above an old high or below an old
  low to fill large orders, then moving price the other way *(7:01)*.
  ICT concept: **liquidity sweep / stop hunt** of buy-side or sell-side liquidity.
- **Bearish TS**: price trades above an old high (taking the buy stops above it),
  then declines *(11:00–12:37)*.
- **Bullish TS**: price trades below an old low (taking the sell stops below it),
  then rallies *(15:20–16:09)*.
- **Old high / old low**: a prior swing high/low from which price has already moved
  away (decline after the high, rally after the low) *(11:00, 15:20)*. No lookback,
  timeframe or swing strength is given.
- **Liquidity above an old high** (= ICT buy-side liquidity) *(11:15–12:05)*:
  (a) stop losses of shorts who sold at or near the high and rode the move down;
  (b) breakout buy orders from traders who treat the high as resistance.
  Mirror for old lows (sell-side liquidity) *(15:20–15:54)*.
- **"Stab"**: a move through the old level. In the drawings it is a wick that pokes
  beyond the level, not a sustained break *(14:15, 15:04)*.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Bearish TS: price must trade above an old high, and must then reverse lower
      ("stab into the old high and dump") *(14:15)*.
- [ ] Bullish TS: price must trade below an old low, then reverse higher *(16:09)*.
- [ ] Direction follows "trapped" traders. Price moves the intended way only after
      the people positioned for that direction are stopped out *(19:16–19:32)*.
- [ ] Must not "pattern trade" TS (buying every old low / selling every old high).
      This works only partly *(19:32–20:07)*.
- [ ] Filters to be detailed later *(20:07–20:39)*:
  - buy low, sell high (premium/discount; ICT **premium/discount**);
  - understand "market profiles" (next episode);
  - use a TS entry model (later episodes);
  - always check higher-timeframe price action (ICT **HTF bias**);
  - always manage risk.
- [ ] Timing matters more than price: "we trade time first then price" *(1:23)*.
      The rules for this come later in the series.

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: no model in this video. An experienced trader can "enter ... right in the
  wick" of the stab *(17:20–17:37)*. Beginners should watch TS happen many times
  before trading it *(17:53–19:16)*.
- Stop: not given (promised later, 1:38).
- Target: not given. The drawings simply show the move back toward the opposite side.
- Timeframe pairs used: not specified. Always check HTF price action *(20:23)*.
- Time-of-day / session filters: none yet. Timing is promised for later episodes *(1:23)*.

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **What counts as an "old high/low"**. Proposal: a fractal swing point (N=2 bars on
  each side) on the reference TF that has not yet been traded through. It must be
  followed by a move of at least X×ATR away from it.
- **How far the stab must go, and how fast it must reverse**. Proposal: high > old
  high by at least 1 tick, and the same candle (or within K candles) closes back
  below the old high. This matches "wick above, close back in" in video 02.
- **Telling TS from a real breakout**. The video says only "check HTF" and "buy low
  sell high". Proposal: valid only if (a) the sweep is in the HTF premium for a sell
  or the discount for a buy (above/below the 50% of the HTF range), and (b) it agrees
  with the HTF bias rule in note 02.
- **"Market profiles"**: undefined here (next episode).
