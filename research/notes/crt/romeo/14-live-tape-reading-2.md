## Summary
An unplanned, vertical (phone) recording of Romeo watching an open short on the
lower timeframe while price heads to a target: relatively equal lows at about
1.1651, which looks like EURUSD. His main point is that just before price reaches a
major liquidity pool it fakes a reversal (bounces, and even a "fake" market
structure shift), and then drops straight into the target. The target was hit
"right at 9:30". He also gives a ranking of journaling methods and says trading is
"80% behavior and 20% technicals". Little rule content; mostly trade management
and psychology.

## Definitions
- **Target / major liquidity pool**: relatively equal lows (two lows, plus a third
  nearby). *(0:52 to 1:08)*
- **Fake MSS near target**: a market structure shift against the trade direction
  that forms just before a pending target is reached. He says it will be "completely
  ignored". *(1:42 to 1:59)*
- **Tape reading**: anticipating, candle by candle on the LTF, how price will move
  toward the target. *(2:34, 5:07)*
- **Bearish market profile (continuation)**: on the way down, highs keep getting
  turtle-souped and lows keep breaking. This continues as long as the target is
  still below. *(12:03 to 12:34)*
- **Journaling levels (lowest to highest)**: notes before/during/after the trade,
  then pictures, then notes on the pictures, then emotions, then a screen recording
  of the trade. *(4:18 to 5:07)*
- ICT concepts used: **liquidity pool / equal lows (sell-side liquidity)**, **draw
  on liquidity**, **turtle soup**, **MSS**.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] While an unmet target (liquidity pool) is pending, you must treat bounces and
      counter-direction MSS close to it as fake, and hold the position. *(1:08, 1:59, 2:17)*
- [ ] Expect the final move into the target to be fast ("viciously dump straight
      down"). He estimated 10–15 minutes, up to about 30 minutes "if they're late". *(1:24, 1:59)*
- [ ] Bearish continuation is valid while highs are being turtle-souped and lows are
      being broken. It ends when that pattern stops (lows hold / highs break). *(12:18 to 12:34)*
- [ ] He watched the 30-minute candle close ("the half hour candle closes in five
      minutes") as a timing reference. *(9:02)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **"Relatively equal lows"**: proposal: ≥ 2 swing lows within 0.1×ATR(14) (or a
  few pips) of each other, unswept.
- **"Close to the target"**: proposal: within 0.5×ATR(14) of the target on the
  entry TF.
- **Fake vs real MSS**: his rule is context-only (target still pending). Proposal
  for the bot: ignore counter-trend LTF MSS signals while price is within X ATR of
  an unswept HTF target, and do not exit early on them.
- **Timing (9:30)**: proposal: test whether targets are often reached around 09:30
  New York time (equity open), and log the timezone in our data.
