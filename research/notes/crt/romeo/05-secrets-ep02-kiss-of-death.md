## Summary
This episode defines the "kiss of death turtle soup" (KoD TS). It is the last
turtle soup (false break of a swing high or low) before price reaches the CRT target.
It is shown on one bearish daily CRT that Romeo says he called live. Inside the
CRT range, the order is: turtle soup of the CRT high, a drop to the 50%
(target 1), a bounce, a final sweep of a local high (the KoD), then a fast move to
the CRT low (target 2). The entry is "model #1" on the lower timeframe.
The rest of the video is promo (book, $1M "bounty", attacks on other mentors).

## Definitions
- **CRT (3-candle)**: candle 1 sets the range (range high / range low). Candle 2
  sweeps one side ("turtle soups" it). Price then attacks the opposite side.
  Romeo maps this to accumulation / manipulation / distribution (ICT **PO3 / AMD**). *(3:28–3:58)*
- **CRT high / CRT low**: the high and low of candle 1. In the bearish example,
  the CRT high is swept and the CRT low is the final target. *(6:36–6:52)*
- **Targets**: target 1 = the 50% level of the candle-1 range. Target 2 = the
  opposite extreme of the candle-1 range (CRT low for a bearish CRT). *(6:52–7:08)*
- **Turtle soup (TS)**: price trades beyond a prior high or low (a liquidity pool) and then
  reverses. It is a false breakout, not a true breakout. Romeo says a TS
  comes before every large move ("the fuel"). Without one you get chop. *(8:12–10:24)*
- **Kiss of death turtle soup**: "the final turtle soup before the target is hit."
  It is usually followed by a fast, vicious move into the target. *(6:20, 7:08–7:23)*
- **Mechanism (ICT liquidity / buy-side & sell-side liquidity)**: price is pushed above an
  engineered high. This stops out shorts and pulls in breakout longs. Then price
  reverses. *(7:41–8:45)*
- **Fake PD arrays**: Romeo says market makers now fade popular ICT patterns, so
  some FVGs and other PD arrays are "fake". *(11:48–12:22)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Higher-TF candle must be monthly, weekly or daily (he trades mostly weekly
      and daily). *(2:53, 6:03)*
- [ ] TF pairs ("golden rule"): Monthly→Daily, Weekly→4H, Daily→1H. *(6:03)*
- [ ] Bearish KoD setup sequence (on the LTF inside the HTF CRT): (1) TS above the CRT
      high, (2) a drop to the 50% of the candle-1 range, with a bounce often there, (3) a rally that
      sweeps a recent local high. This rally is often a big up-close candle. (4) Reversal = KoD, (5) target the CRT low.
      *(7:23, 10:43, 14:35–15:12)*
- [ ] Confluence: a bearish FVG sitting above an old high (or a bullish FVG below an old
      low) supports the KoD sell. *(17:36–18:12)*
- [ ] Location: Romeo prefers the KoD in the "lower 25% of the range". It can also
      occur at an OTE retracement (ICT **OTE**). *(19:16)*
- [ ] Bullish KoD is the mirror image. It was left as homework, so it is not shown. *(19:49)*
- [ ] The trade is taken only after the entry model triggers. Entry model is always
      the last step. *(18:27)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: "model number one" on the lower timeframe at the KoD sweep (model #1
  is defined in ep.1/ep.6: close beyond the candle that swept the level). *(18:27)*
- Stop: above the KoD sweep high ("risk up here"). *(18:44)*
- Target: CRT low (target 2). The 50% is target 1. He notes equal lows left as "bait"
  on the way down. *(7:08, 18:44)*
- Timeframe pairs used: M→D, W→4H, D→1H. Example: daily CRT, executed on 1H. *(5:43–6:03)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: none in this video.

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **Which TS is "the final" one**: you only know this in hindsight. Proposal: inside an
  active CRT (after the candle-1 extreme is swept and the 50% is touched), any
  sweep of the most recent LTF swing high (bearish case) that has a bearish FVG
  above it and no new candle-1 high is a KoD candidate.
- **"Lower 25% of the range"**: this is ambiguous for a bearish setup. It may mean
  the 25% of the candle-1 range nearest the target. Measure the sweep high's position as a % of the
  candle-1 range from the target side and log it. Test whether <=25% performs better.
- **"Vicious, fast move"**: define it as reaching the target within N LTF bars (calibrate).
- **"Massive bulging up-close candle"**: define it as a candle body >= k x ATR(14) on that TF.
- **Fake FVG vs real FVG**: no criterion is given. Drop it or treat it as a filter to test later.
