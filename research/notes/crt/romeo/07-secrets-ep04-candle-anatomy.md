## Summary
This is a mostly conceptual episode. The first decision is always which HTF candle you are trading.
Only 4H, daily and weekly are allowed (monthly and above for advanced students). All candles
form the same way at every timeframe (fractal). The core pattern is: open, a move against the intended
direction (turtle soup of a CRT), then expansion. LTF charts are for analysis practice, not
for trading. No chart example is shown; an Ethereum breakdown was skipped. Large parts are
anti-scalping / anti-gambling talk.

## Definitions
- **Candle**: defined by its opening time, its closing time, and the price action between them. *(4:43–5:02)*
- **Fractal nature of price**: every candle (1H to 12M) prints with "the exact same
  mechanisms". Only the speed differs. You time highs and lows the same way on any TF. *(6:40–7:30)*
- **Bearish candle anatomy**: open, rally into a key level, turtle soup of a CRT, then dump.
  This is the ICT **PO3 / AMD** daily candle profile. *(8:40–8:57, 14:42–14:58)*
- **Bullish candle anatomy**: open, drop, turtle soup of a CRT, then pump. *(14:58–15:14)*
- Called "the strongest pattern that exists in technical analysis". *(14:42)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] First step, before any pattern or entry: choose the HTF candle being traded. *(5:33–5:49, 14:26)*
- [ ] Tradable HTF candles: 4H, Daily, Weekly only. Beginner: 4H. Intermediate: Daily.
      Advanced: Weekly (the order is implied by his drawing). *(6:05–6:24, 8:06–8:23)*
- [ ] Monthly, 3M, 6M and 12M are allowed only once advanced. Even advanced traders should stick to
      higher TFs. *(6:24, 8:23–8:40)*
- [ ] Do not trade candles below 4H (e.g. a 5m candle as the HTF). *(6:05)*
- [ ] Bearish setup: price must first rally after the candle opens, reach a key level, and sweep (turtle soup) a CRT
      high before you look for shorts. Mirror the logic for bullish setups. *(8:40–8:57, 14:42–15:14)*
- [ ] Low trade frequency: about one or two trades a week, at most about one a day. *(11:17)*
- [ ] The entry model is the last thing considered. *(5:33)*
