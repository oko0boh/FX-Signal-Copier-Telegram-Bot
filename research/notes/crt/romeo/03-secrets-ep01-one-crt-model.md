## Summary
This is the first episode of "CRT Secrets", the 2025 free public mentorship. It
teaches "model number one": a single-candle lower-timeframe entry after price sweeps
an old high or low that sits at a higher-timeframe level. The model is applied
multi-timeframe, with specific HTF candle → LTF model pairs. It adds two probability
filters: an FVG next to the model candle, and weekly "time theory" (high of week on
Mon–Wed, opposite extreme Thu–Fri). The first ~10 minutes are about who originated
CRT (he says he did) and are skipped.

## Definitions
- **Model #1, bearish** *(9:59–10:52, 15:05–15:23)*: there is an old high. Price
  "stabs" above it with a **thick up-close candle** (the model candle). The trigger
  is a later candle **closing below** that model candle. Then enter short.
- **Model #1, bullish** *(10:52, 15:23–15:38, 17:52–19:48)*: price stabs below an
  old low with a **thick down-close candle**. The trigger is a close **above** that
  candle. Then enter long.
- **"One specific candle"**: the model is a single candle, "not a zone, not a bunch
  of candles" *(19:13, 21:28–21:45)*. ICT concept: like an **order block** taken as
  one candle, but triggered by a close through it.
- **Real support/resistance**: only an old high or old low. Ordinary S/R levels in
  between are "smashed" on the way *(18:58–19:13)*. ICT concept: **liquidity**
  (buy-side / sell-side).
- **Weekly candle (time theory)** *(24:03–24:35)*: weekly open = **Monday 00:00**
  (NY assumed), weekly close = **Friday 17:00**. For crypto, use Monday–Friday too.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] The level must be an old high (bearish) or old low (bullish), taken from the
      **HTF candle / key level** of the pair (see TF pairs) *(17:52–18:10, 28:25)*.
- [ ] The model candle must be **thick**. Not being selective about thick candles is
      "the mistake I see you all make" *(11:42–12:01)*.
- [ ] Must wait for a **candle close** beyond the model candle. A wick through it
      does not count, because "sometimes they will wick above the candle and then
      dump" *(22:01, 27:04)*.
- [ ] Higher probability if the model candle comes with an **FVG** (fair value gap)
      *(15:59, 20:38–21:12, 27:53)*.
- [ ] Higher probability if it forms "at a certain time" *(16:16)*. Time filter
      *(23:11–26:28)*:
  - the high of the week forms Mon/Tue/Wed (bearish week); the opposite extreme
    (low) is reached Thu/Fri;
  - typical sequence: fake high on Monday (often with an FVG to trap traders), real
    high on Tuesday with model #1, then decline to Thu/Fri;
  - "Thursday plus model one" = highest-probability point (time and price meet)
    *(26:28)*;
  - "If the time is right, the pattern will work. If the time is wrong, the pattern
    will fail." *(23:28)* ICT concept: **weekly profiles / time of day of HOW-LOW**.
- [ ] Be selective. Do not force trades *(14:46, 22:19–22:51)*.

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: when the close beyond the model candle happens ("as soon as they close,
  that's the trigger") *(20:04)*. For bullish, "entry is the high of model number
  one" *(20:20)*: buy at or above the model candle's high once a candle closes above
  it.
- Stop: the opposite extreme of the model candle. Bullish = model candle low
  *(20:20)*; bearish = above the high *(10:34)*. Narrower stops exist but are not
  taught publicly *(19:48–20:04)*.
- Target: "target the lows one by one" for bearish, "the highs one by one" for
  bullish. That means successive opposing swing points / liquidity *(10:34,
  19:48, 22:01)*.
- Timeframe pairs used (HTF candle → LTF model #1) *(16:49–17:22, 28:25–28:41)*:
  Monthly → Daily, Weekly → 4H, Daily → 1H, 4H → 15m. Lower pairs exist but are
  not public.
- Time-of-day / session filters: weekly time theory as above. No intraday killzone
  is given in this episode.

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **"Thick" candle**. Proposal: body/range ≥ 0.6 and range ≥ 1×ATR(14) on the LTF,
  or the largest body of the last N candles.
- **"Comes with an FVG"**. Proposal: a standard 3-candle FVG (candle[i-1].low >
  candle[i+1].high for bearish) where the model candle or the trigger candle is the
  middle candle, formed within 3 bars of the trigger.
- **"At a certain time"**. Only the weekly profile is concrete. Proposal: a bearish
  model #1 is valid only if its high forms Mon–Wed, and a long-side reversal is
  looked for Thu–Fri. Measure the hit rate per weekday.
- **Which old high**: an HTF candle's high vs any LTF swing high. Proposal: the
  previous HTF candle's high/low (C1 of note 02), or an unswept HTF swing point.
- **"Target one by one"**: how many, and where to take partials. Proposal: TP1 =
  nearest opposing LTF fractal swing, TP2 = 50% of the HTF candle, TP3 = opposite
  HTF extreme (combining note 02).

## Conflicts with other sources
- **TF pairs** differ from note 02 (there: M→4H, W→1H, D→15m, 4H→5m).
- **Weekly open**: here Monday 00:00 open / Friday 17:00 close. Note 02 uses the
  Sunday 17:00 open for the weekly C1 range but also calls Monday "true week open".
- **Target**: "highs/lows one by one" here vs "50% then opposite end of C1" in note 02.
- **Trigger body vs wick**: here "entry is the high of model #1" (implies the wick
  high). Note 02 says "ignore the wicks look at the bodies".
