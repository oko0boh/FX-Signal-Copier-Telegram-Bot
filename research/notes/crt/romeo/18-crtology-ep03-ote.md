## Summary
Romeo redefines ICT's **OTE (optimal trade entry)**. For him it is not a Fibonacci level.
It is a structural pattern: a swing from a key level, a **close** through the
"low that broke the high", then a retracement into roughly the 50–80% of
leg one, then a second expansion leg. *(1:59–9:45)* He shows one chart example of a
bearish OTE (instrument and date not shown). The zone is drawn as a 0.5–0.79 fib of leg
one, also measured on bodies. Targets are the body of the high that started the
leg, then the CRT 50%, then the CRT high/low. (Promo/personal: pause due to a family
death; "rewatch".)

## Definitions
- **OTE (bearish form)**: a sequence high → low → higher high (into a **key level**),
  then price **breaks the low that broke the high**. *(1:59, 3:23–3:39)*
- **OTE activation**: only when a candle **closes** below that low. A wick through it
  does **not** activate the OTE. *(2:16–2:32, 5:01–5:17)* Activation means "start
  seeking" an OTE, not "sell now". *(2:16)*
- **Preferred break**: a "wave" (several pushes/closes) through the low, rather than
  one or two closes. Then a sharp retracement back up. *(2:32–2:50)*
- **What the OTE is**: "the decline after a true market structure shift stemming from
  a key level, designed to scare away everyone before rallying into the second leg"
  *(9:28–9:45)*. Bullish wording, but the example is bearish; the pattern is symmetric. It is the
  retracement after leg one into "more or less the 50% … up until the 80%". *(8:54–9:11)*
- **Leg one / leg two**: leg one = the impulse that breaks the key low. The OTE trader
  trades **leg two**. *(11:39–11:58)*
- **OTE zone drawing**: fib from the high that broke the low (the swing high) to the end
  of expansion leg one. Zone = 0.5 to 0.79. A second fib is drawn on **candle bodies**
  as well. *(12:34–13:08)*
- **CRT link**: the key level at the origin is the **CRT low** (bullish) or high. *(10:50)*
- **Retracement cause**: liquidity, i.e. a run on buy stops above a nearby high, inducing
  buyers, before the reversal. Not the fib or FVG itself. *(6:38–7:12)*
- **CRT at the top of the retracement**: a LTF CRT marks where the retracement tops out. *(7:45–8:04, 14:16)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Structure must be: swing high, swing low, higher high into a key level (bearish case;
      mirror for bullish). *(3:23)*
- [ ] Activation needs a candle **close** beyond the low that broke the high. A wick-only break
      must not activate it. *(5:01)*
- [ ] Prefer multiple closes / a wave through the low. *(2:32)*
- [ ] Wait for a retracement into the 0.5–0.79 zone of leg one. A fixed fib level (0.618/0.62/0.66
      "golden pocket") must **not** be required. *(4:10, 5:34–6:07, 12:51)*
- [ ] Preferred reaction: the lower half of the zone (shallow, for bearish: 0.5–~0.65). A
      shallow retrace means "heaviness" and a strong continuation. Deeper = "more troublesome".
      *(13:45–14:16)*
- [ ] Look for a LTF CRT (and a liquidity grab of nearby buy stops) at the retracement top. *(7:45, 14:16)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: in the 0.5–0.79 zone (bodies fib as a refinement), on a LTF CRT/turn. *(13:26–14:00)*
- Stop: not stated. Logically above the retracement high / above the 0.79 level (to be confirmed).
- Target: (1) the **body** of the high that started the decline into the zone, the "most
  guaranteed" target. *(10:02–10:18)* Then (2) previous highs/lows. (3) The 50% of the
  CRT range. *(11:06–11:39)* (4) The CRT high/low as an extended target. *(11:06)*
- Timeframe pairs used: not given. The chart's TF is not stated; "lower time frames"
  show the retracement as a full bull market with a CRT at its top. *(7:45)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: none in this episode.

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **Swing definitions** (high, low, higher high): proposal: fractal N=2–3 on the working TF.
- **"Low that broke the high"**: the swing low formed between the first high and the
  higher high. Must be implemented as the last swing low before the HH.
- **"End of leg one"**: proposal: the lowest low after activation, before the first
  retracement closes above a k·ATR threshold or before a LTF MSS up.
- **"Wave" through the low**: count closes beyond the level, e.g. ≥ 2 closes or ≥ 2 LTF
  impulses.
- **"Sharp" retracement**: proposal: a retracement of ≥ 0.5 of leg one within ≤ M bars.
- **Key level**: still undefined (CRT high/low is used here; the key-level episode is pending).
- **Body fib vs wick fib**: how to combine them is unclear. Proposal: zone = union, or
  intersection, of the 0.5–0.79 bands from both, and test both.

## Conflicts with other sources
- Contradicts standard ICT OTE (0.62–0.79 fib entry with 0.705 sweet spot): Romeo uses
  0.5–0.79. He rejects a fixed fib level as the reason for the turn and sees the
  OTE as a structural pattern. *(5:34–6:07, 9:11)*
- Ep.3 wording "the 50% to the 80%" *(9:11)* vs drawing 0.5–0.79 *(12:51)*. These are
  effectively the same.
- Ep.3 needs a **close** through the structural low to activate. Ep.2 calls the LTF trigger
  a "market structure shift" without spelling out close vs wick. Assume a close is
  needed in both.
