## Summary
Sham gives a four-step framework for ICT **daily bias**: (1) structure and order flow,
(2) relevant dealing range and its PDAs, (3) short-term vs long-term draws on
liquidity, using the weekly and monthly OHLC and SMT, (4) the final bias
(bullish / bearish / neutral) from the OHLC frameworks, SMT and timing. Bias is set
**per isolated day**, so it can go against the HTF structure while a dealing range is
rebalanced. A long USDCAD walk-through shows the bias day by day over several weeks.
The video closes with FTMO stats and Telegram promotion.

## Definitions
- **Daily bias**: the expectation of where price will trade today and what kind of
  delivery it will produce. Values: bullish / bearish / neutral (risk off). *(1:20–1:52)*
- **Three questions** a bias must answer: where is price going (DOL), how will it get
  there (path / key level), which direction to trade today. *(2:45–5:42)*
- **Internal vs external range liquidity**: short-term DOL inside the range (FVG,
  equilibrium) vs the range high/low. *(3:16–3:31)*
- **Relevant dealing range**: the most recent **expansion swing**, but only if it holds
  **unfilled liquidity** (untested FVG/OB/PDA). *(14:19–14:51)*
- **Dealing range confirmation**: the expansion swing becomes a dealing range only after
  a **CSD (change in state of delivery)** in the counter direction. An SMT on top makes it
  stronger. The CSD can be on **D1 or H4 (H4 is the lowest allowed)** for a D1 range.
  *(15:55–16:29, 34:41–35:14)*
- **CSD (as shown)**: a candle purges the previous low, then a later candle closes above
  (engulfs) the candles that purged. *(35:31–35:47)*
- **Monthly/weekly OHLC rule**: before a monthly (weekly) candle makes its meaningful
  expansion, it first forms its opposite extreme (the high for a bearish candle) at a
  HTF key level. *(21:15–22:03, 28:03–28:18)*
- **Tuesday low of the week**: Monday ranges, Tuesday sweeps the previous week's low
  (with SMT), Wednesday closes bullish (CSD). Expect a continuation on Thursday. *(23:41–24:28)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Step 1: structure bearish if it rejects above old highs and breaks below old lows
  (lower highs, lower lows, bearish range expansions); the mirror for bullish. *(13:45–14:02)*
- [ ] Step 2: if the latest expansion swing has **no** unfilled liquidity, skip the dealing
  range and go straight to step 4. *(14:51–15:40)*
- [ ] Step 2: if it has unfilled liquidity, wait for a counter CSD (+SMT) before you use
  it as the dealing range. *(15:55–16:29)*
- [ ] Step 3: short-term DOL is at least the premium FVG / equilibrium of the dealing
  range; long-term DOL is the external range low (for bearish structure). *(18:36–19:07)*
- [ ] Step 4: bias = the direction of the short-term objective, if the monthly OHLC,
  weekly OHLC and SMT agree. *(19:23–20:12, 26:05)*
- [ ] Opposing SMT on a correlated asset (DXY for USDCAD) is a conflicting factor. It can
  make the day **neutral**. *(25:15–25:48)*
- [ ] Opposing SMT can be **overridden** when the weekly/monthly key levels are not yet
  reached and a new weekly candle must first form its high/low. *(27:44–28:36)*
- [ ] A new weekly candle **cannot** expand straight away in the trend direction: it first
  forms its high (bearish week) at a key level. "It never happens" otherwise. *(28:03–28:36)*
- [ ] Monday with conflicting information (counter SMT + new week) → **neutral**, risk
  off; "Monday tends to be ranging". *(33:39–34:11)*
- [ ] The bias flips intraday ("midday") once the premium region is reached and the dealing
  range is rebalanced. *(38:12)*
- [ ] Ranging days (e.g. some Fridays) → no trade. *(31:22)*
- [ ] Routine: do the full analysis at the start of the week, then update daily with the
  weekly/monthly OHLC and SMT. *(40:03–40:36)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- "Unfilled liquidity": proposal: any FVG or OB inside the swing whose zone was not
  touched by a later wick.
- "Conflicting information too strong" → neutral: proposal: count bullish vs bearish
  factors (monthly OHLC, weekly OHLC, short-term DOL, SMT). Go neutral on a tie, or when
  a counter SMT is present on a Monday.
- "Key timing" in step 4 is never defined here. See video 04.
- When the override beats a counter SMT is a judgement call. Proposal: an untested weekly
  or monthly PDA within the dealing range beats a D1 SMT.

## Conflicts with other sources
- Video 01 KOD exception: no rebalance needed near the DOL. This video always expects the
  dealing-range fill first.
- Video 06 mentions only the weekly OHLC (not the monthly) in daily bias. Here both are used.
