## Summary
Sham's method for choosing which candle to treat as a CRT (candle range). Before
picking a candle you decide what it is for: a **CRT for bias** (on the daily, used
to set directional bias) or a **CRT for framing trades** (on the 4H, used to frame a
15m entry). Both kinds go through the same three steps: (1) structure + context,
(2) timing, (3) confirmation. Steps 2 and 3 work differently for the two kinds.
He walks through live examples on NQ (daily), USDCAD (weekly/daily) and USDCAD
(4H/15m). (Promo: he says he averages about $25k/month with this method.)

Note: the auto-captions write "buys" for **bias** throughout ("CRT for buys" means
CRT for bias).

## Definitions
- **CRT / candle range**: a higher-timeframe candle whose high and low are treated
  as a range. Price is expected to purge one side and then deliver toward the
  other side. *(0:00, 12:39)*
- **CRT for bias**: a daily candle used to set the higher-timeframe directional
  bias for the next few days. *(1:19, 6:33)*
- **CRT for framing trades**: a 4H candle used to frame an execution on the 15m.
  Sham says this kind matters more than CRT for bias. *(1:34, 18:12, 32:18)*
- **Structure**: the HTF market structure. It gives the **direction**: bullish
  structure → look for bullish CRTs, bearish → bearish. *(2:22, 5:13)*
- **Context**: **location**. Is price inside a bullish or bearish daily dealing
  range, in premium or discount, and near a meaningful HTF key level (FVG, order
  block, previous week high/low)? *(2:53, 5:30)*
- **Daily range / dealing range**: the most recent expansion swing on the TF in
  question. Its 50% is the **equilibrium**. Premium is above it, discount below.
  *(23:31, 33:06)*
- **Confirmation for a CRT for bias**: the **purging candle** (the next daily
  candle that trades through the CRT high or low) must **close back inside** the
  CRT range. Without that close it is "just a liquidity purge". *(12:39–13:26)*
- **Confirmation for a CRT for framing trades**: once the 4H CRT is purged at a
  key time, drop to the **15m** and wait for a change in the state of delivery
  (CSD) or a structure shift, which forms an order block. The 4H candle does
  **not** need to close back inside. *(20:19, 34:09)*
- **Closing hour logic**: watch how the **last hour of the 4H candle** behaves at
  the key level. Sham calls it "one of the most important" timing elements, but
  he does not explain it here (he says it needs a separate lecture). *(19:31)*
- **Better range / inside bars**: if the latest candles sit inside an earlier
  candle, use the **outer (mother) candle** as the CRT, not the inside candles.
  *(30:40)*

## Rules / conditions
Shared steps
- [ ] Must first label the CRT's purpose: bias (daily) or framing (4H). *(1:19, 1:50)*
- [ ] CRT direction must match the HTF structure, **or** the short-term draw
  inside the current dealing range. A bullish CRT inside bearish daily structure
  is allowed when price still has to rebalance a bearish daily range up toward EQ
  or premium. *(5:46–6:16, 23:47–26:23)*
- [ ] The CRT must form at a location where expansion is expected: a premium key
  level for bearish CRTs, discount for bullish, or near a HTF FVG, OB or previous
  week high/low. *(2:53, 8:24–9:28)*
- [ ] Good timing or confirmation does not rescue a candle in the wrong context.
  Fail step 1 and you stop. *(6:01)*
- [ ] Do not take a candle just because it sits at a key level in the right
  structure. The candle must fit the current dealing-range context: price must
  first rebalance the daily range before a candle in the main direction is
  selected. *(22:44–24:18)*
- [ ] Special case: at all-time highs with no dealing range to rebalance, just
  wait for a sweep of a nearby low (here, last week's low, which lines up with a
  weekly bullish FVG). Do not wait for a deep retracement. *(7:36–8:24)*
- [ ] A bearish engulfing candle at highs inside bullish structure is the reason
  to expect its low to be purged first. Without it, keep expecting continuation.
  *(9:12–9:28)*

CRT for bias (daily): timing
- [ ] Check weekly profiles: is the week in expansion or retracement, and has the
  high or low of the week formed? *(11:05)*
- [ ] Check weekly OHLC: where does the candle sit inside the weekly candle? Is it
  forming before the real weekly expansion or after it? *(11:37)*
- [ ] Check monthly OHLC: has the monthly candle already expanded, and what draw
  is it reaching for? *(11:52–12:23)*
- [ ] **Monday's daily candle** is the preferred CRT for bias. It sets up a
  Tuesday or Wednesday low (or high) of the week. A Thursday or Friday CRT has
  ambiguous timing. *(14:14–15:51, 27:45–28:17)*
- [ ] Wednesday can also be the CRT in a midweek retracement profile (USDCAD
  example). *(26:23)*

CRT for bias: confirmation and invalidation
- [ ] The purging candle must close back inside the CRT. Then bias flips toward
  the opposite side of the range for the next few days. *(16:21–17:24)*
- [ ] If the purging candle closes outside the range (beyond it), the CRT is
  rejected. Adapt the bias and wait for another candidate. *(17:39, 28:50)*

CRT for framing trades (4H): timing and confirmation
- [ ] Check the daily profile: is the day forming its high or low first, and is
  it in a retracement or an expansion profile? *(18:12–18:43)*
- [ ] The 4H CRT should form at a key time **and must be purged at a key time**
  (session, killzone or macro). *(18:59–19:14)*
- [ ] After the purge, wait for price to also rebalance the daily dealing range
  (reach EQ or premium). A purge before the rebalance is ignored, even if a
  bearish engulfing candle appears. *(35:44–36:00)*
- [ ] Then require a 15m CSD that forms an order block. *(20:19, 36:16)*
- [ ] If no 15m confirmation forms, or price keeps going against the idea, the 4H
  CRT is invalid: stay risk-off. *(38:07–38:56)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: at the 15m order block formed by the CSD after the purge of the 4H CRT.
  *(37:36)*
- Stop: just beyond the order block (above it for shorts). *(37:52)*
- Target: a fixed **3R** in the 4H example. *(37:52)* The bias CRT targets the
  opposite side of the range or the dealing-range EQ or premium. *(25:53, 29:22)*
- Timeframe pairs used: weekly → daily (CRT for bias); daily bias → 4H CRT → 15m
  confirmation.
- Time-of-day / session filters: London open killzone. The 4H candle at
  **9:00 p.m.** (NY time) is the CRT, and the next 4H candle opens into London.
  The **3:00 a.m. macro** forms the high of the day: London open high of day,
  which is a "normal protraction" profile with the Asian range below. *(33:22,
  34:41, 36:16, 37:04)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **Weekly or daily profile**: deciding which profile the week or day is in.
  Proposed rule: weekly is "Monday range" if Monday's range is less than X% of
  the ADR × 5; look up profile probabilities from historical weekday-of-high or
  low stats.
- **Context fit / "makes sense"**: proposed rule: a bearish CRT needs its high
  ≥ the dealing-range EQ (premium). Bullish needs its low ≤ EQ. A candidate is
  valid only once price has touched EQ of the most recent expansion swing.
- **Most recent expansion swing (dealing range)**: needs a swing-detection
  algorithm (e.g. a fractal of N bars, plus displacement ≥ k·ATR).
- **CSD / structure shift on the 15m**: proposed rule: a candle closes beyond the
  open of the last opposite-colour candle run (the order block), as in ICT
  "change in state of delivery".
- **"Meaningful key level"**: limit to FVGs, OBs and previous week or day highs
  and lows on the HTF (as Sham also says in video 10).
- **Closing hour logic**: not defined. Open item.
- **Choosing between competing candidates** (Friday vs Monday vs Thursday):
  proposed rule: prefer the outer candle of inside bars, then the candle closest
  to premium or discount.
