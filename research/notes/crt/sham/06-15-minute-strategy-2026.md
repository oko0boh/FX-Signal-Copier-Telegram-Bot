## Summary
This is Sham's full model in three components: **D1 daily bias → H4 valid candle range
(CRT) inside a context → M15 entry model (OB after a key-time purge)**. Worked examples
cover a week on EURUSD. Monday: a short at London open. Tuesday: a counter-trend long
after the first CRT was invalidated by an off-key-time purge, then a 1 am CRT purged at
the 6 am macro. Wednesday: FOMC, no valid setup, no trade. Thursday: the bias flips
bullish (manipulation profile, KOD) and a long from a 3 am purge. It closes with stats
and promotion ("$25k/month").

## Definitions
- **Daily bias inputs**: D1 structure, current dealing range plus key levels, and the
  **weekly OHLC** of the current week. *(1:51–3:11)*
- **H4 market context**: the current dealing range plus key levels inside it, in the
  direction of the daily bias. *(3:42–4:14)*
- **Valid candle range**: an H4 candle that sits in the context (premium for bearish),
  near the refined key level, aligned with the bias, and that makes sense in time (selected
  at or after the session start). *(9:25–10:12)*
- **Key timing**: macros, killzones, sessions **and** the "closing hour dynamics" of the
  4H candles. *(10:43–10:59)*
- **KOD (kiss of death) sequence**: price has almost reached the DOL, stops, makes new
  liquidity and retraces. That retracement need not rebalance the dealing range and can end
  at a premium key level (in the bullish case). It is "the only exception" to the rebalance
  rule. *(20:36–21:24)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Before the session/killzone: monitor only, stay neutral, select no candle. *(8:52–9:25)*
- [ ] The CRT must be purged **at a key time**. A purge outside key time **invalidates** the
  candle range and the trade idea. *(10:28–10:43, 13:24–14:28)*
- [ ] A valid purge needs both: (1) it happens around a candle range; (2) the timing is
  correct. *(14:11–14:28)*
- [ ] After the purge, do not enter at once. Wait for an OB to form (the entry model). *(6:08–6:29, 11:14)*
- [ ] The dealing range is never fixed. Re-anchor it as price makes new swings. *(12:35–12:50)*
- [ ] A counter-trend trade is allowed when the short-term DOL is the rebalance of a new
  dealing range confirmed by a strong SMT (EU took a major low, GU did not). *(13:07, 14:46–16:06)*
- [ ] After the day's objective is reached: done for the day, close the charts, neutral
  until the next day. *(17:07–17:39)*
- [ ] High-impact news (FOMC) day → risk off. *(17:54–18:11)*
- [ ] No valid purge or entry → no trade, even if the bias is still held. The bias "can change
  with just a single candle". *(18:27–18:42)*
- [ ] Bias update: a strong counter D1 candle plus a 4H expansion means the expansion
  profile is over and a manipulation profile has begun. Flip the bias toward the deeper
  retracement target (premium FVG / high). *(19:00–20:05)*
- [ ] Take the deeper (valid) purge: the initial 2 am sweep was followed by a lower sweep at
  3 am, which was the valid one. *(22:13–22:29)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: at the OB edge (shorts: OB low; longs: "at the high of this order block") after
  the OB forms. A refined single-candle OB is also used. *(11:14–11:32, 16:52, 22:45–23:00)*
- Stop: beyond the OB (above the OB high for shorts, below for longs). *(11:32, 16:52)*
- Target: "1 to 3R" *(11:32, 16:52)*, typically 3R *(23:00)*. In the counter trade the
  target matched the dealing-range equilibrium. *(16:52)*
- Timeframe pairs used: D1 → H4 → M15. *(1:19–1:35)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: London open (03:00 macro); the 6 am macro for the Tuesday
  purge; the 1 am NY 4H candle was used as the CRT. *(15:18–15:33, 21:56)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- "Closing hour dynamics of the 4H candles" is not explained. Proposal: a purge counts more
  when it happens in the first hour of a new H4 candle (e.g. the 05:00 open) or in its last
  hour. Needs another source.
- "Which candle objectively makes the most sense": proposal as in note 05 (the last closed
  H4 candle overlapping the key level in the correct half of the range at the session start).
- When a strong counter candle means a manipulation profile (bias flip): proposal: a D1 close
  beyond the prior day's range in the counter direction plus an H4 CSD.
- Which purge is "the valid one" when there are several in a killzone: proposal: the purge
  inside a macro hour, or the extreme reached by the close of the killzone.
