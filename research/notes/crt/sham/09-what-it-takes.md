## Summary
A short talk on mindset. Sham shows that he took only **5 trades in about a
month** (all wins) and argues that trade frequency does not matter, only positive
expectancy and consistent execution. The style you trade should suit your
psychology: he does not scalp. He also says to re-derive bias every day and not
"marry" an old narrative. (Promo for a September 3rd Telegram launch.)

## Definitions
- **Fragmented / incomplete edge**: you know the concepts (top-down analysis,
  entries, context, narrative), but not every rule or scenario is defined. That
  includes no-trade and invalidation conditions. *(4:47–5:33)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Low frequency is fine. Do not force trades to raise the count. *(0:00–1:22)*
- [ ] Rebuild the bias daily from new price information. Drop a narrative once
  it is invalidated. *(7:12, 9:52)*
- [ ] Judge bias day by day. Four correct bullish days are still valid even if
  day 5 reverses the week. *(7:57–9:02)*
