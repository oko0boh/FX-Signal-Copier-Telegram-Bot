## Summary
Sham defines CRT as treating one higher-timeframe candle as a "true range" when it forms
under the right market conditions (structure, dealing range) and the right context (key
level, key time). Its main use is to tell you **which** liquidity purge is valid: only a
purge of a *closed* CRT candle's high/low, at a key time, at the HTF key level. He says
CRT has little value on its own; ICT supplies the context. He names two CRT types: a
D1/W1 CRT for bias and an H4 CRT for context. Entries are LTF OB/FVG (+SMT). The candle
count of the pattern does not matter. The opening P&L claims are promotion.

## Definitions
- **CRT**: "a higher time frame candle can be treated as a range or a true range" under the
  right context and market conditions. *(1:34–1:51)*
- **Market conditions**: HTF structure plus price inside a dealing range in that direction. *(2:39–2:54)*
- **Market context**: the candle formed in the correct premium/discount half of the dealing
  range, at a key level, around a key time. *(2:54–3:10)*
- **Core premise**: true expansions happen only after liquidity above/below a true range is
  taken **and** a HTF key level is tested. *(12:48–13:05)*
- **Valid purge**: price trades beyond the high (bearish) or low (bullish) of the CRT candle
  **after that candle has closed**. Purges while the CRT candle is still forming are not
  valid. *(4:14–4:31, 15:12–15:46)*
- **CRT for bias**: selected on **D1 / W1**. It is confirmed when price purges the candle
  range and **closes back inside** it. Then keep the bias toward the opposite end (e.g. the
  CRT low) until it is reached. *(25:25–25:41, 26:27–27:45)*
- **CRT for context**: selected on **H4**, to frame trades within the existing bias. When
  it is purged in context, drop to the LTF for entry. *(25:41–25:57)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Build the ICT context first (structure → daily bias → session/key time → H4 dealing
  range + key level), then select the CRT candle. You must not pick a candle first.
  *(9:04–9:36, 24:04–24:19)*
- [ ] Bias bearish → consider only bearish CRTs (purge of a high); ignore bullish ones. *(5:51–6:06)*
- [ ] Do not trade, or select candles, during the Asian range or midnight. Start at the
  London open. *(6:22–6:54)*
- [ ] The CRT candle must sit at the premium key level (bearish) or discount key level
  (bullish) of the H4 dealing range. *(7:10, 8:30)*
- [ ] Price tagging the key level before the CRT exists, or outside key time → no entry,
  even if an LTF OB/CSD forms. *(3:26–4:14, 17:38–18:58)*
- [ ] Repeated sweeps of lesser highs/lows inside a forming CRT candle are invalid. *(14:25–15:46, 20:32–21:36)*
- [ ] Example timing: the CRT high purged at the **03:00 macro** after the London open began. *(19:14–19:30)*
- [ ] Alternative scenario: if price closes below the candles and forms a new OB
  ("propulsion block"), select the candle that formed it (the 05:00 NY 4H candle). In
  New York, expect a purge of its high into the OB and then a "New York continuation". *(11:11–12:16)*
- [ ] Number of candles in the CRT pattern (2, 3, 4, 5) does not matter. Context and
  timing are primary; the candle is secondary. *(28:33–29:52)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: after the CRT high/low is purged at a key time, wait for a LTF OB (price
  engulfs the candle(s) that purged the CRT) or an FVG. SMT is a plus. *(28:03–28:33)*
- Stop: beyond the OB (above for shorts). *(22:08)*
- Target: 3R *(22:08)*; or the dealing-range low / DOL *(12:16)*.
- Timeframe pairs used: D1 (structure, bias) → H4 CRT + context → M15 entry. D1/W1 CRT
  for bias. *(17:23, 25:25)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: London open session and New York killzone. Ignore Asia.

## Vague / discretionary parts
- "Which candle makes the most sense": not fully objective (he promises a follow-up video).
  Proposal: the latest *closed* H4 candle before or at the killzone start whose range
  overlaps the HTF key level and lies in the correct premium/discount half.
- "Around a key time" for the CRT candle itself is not quantified.
- Normal vs delayed protraction / "propulsion block" scenarios are not defined. Log them as
  ICT intraday profiles to be defined later.

## Conflicts with other sources
- Classic CRT teaching (Romeo and others) uses the 3-candle pattern. Sham says the candle
  count is irrelevant.
- Video 02 gives OB entry at the OB "high/open". Here the OB is "price engulfing the
  candle(s) that purged the CRT". Same concept.
