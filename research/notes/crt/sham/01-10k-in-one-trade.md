## Summary
Sham breaks down one GBPUSD (GU) short taken on a Wednesday on an FTMO account
(about 50 pips; he says roughly 3–6 hours). The trade follows his three-step model:
**daily bias on D1 → market context (key level + 4H candle range) on H4 → entry
confirmation (order block, ideally with SMT) on M15**. The main idea is the
"kiss of death" (KOD, spelled "QOD" in the captions) sequence: when price is
already very close to the higher-timeframe draw on liquidity, the last retracement
can end at a *discount* key level. In that case price does not need to rebalance the
dealing range back up to premium. The rest of the video is FTMO promotion.

## Definitions
- **Daily bias**: which direction price is most likely to expand today; must be set
  first, on D1. If the direction is wrong, the other steps do not matter. *(0:48–1:22)*
- **Market context (H4)**: an appropriate key level plus a 4H candle range (CRT)
  that the trade is framed around. *(1:39–1:54, 2:25)*
- **Market profiles**: expansion, retracement, consolidation, manipulation. Trade only
  in the expansion profile toward the HTF draw on liquidity (DOL). *(2:09, 5:56–6:12)*
- **Dealing range (D1)**: the most recent *expansion swing* (swing high to swing low
  for bearish). Mark the premium and discount key levels (FVGs) inside it. *(7:00–7:48)*
- **Standard sequence**: after a dealing range forms, price first rebalances it
  (retraces to equilibrium or premium in a bearish range), tests a premium key level,
  then expands toward the DOL. Bullish is the mirror. *(8:36–9:25)*
- **KOD / "kiss of death" sequence**: ICT term; Sham calls it "the last distribution
  leg inside of a bearish market maker model". It applies when price has *almost*
  reached the HTF DOL, pauses, forms SMT (here with DXY), engineers liquidity and
  makes a small retracement. That final retracement "almost always ends inside the
  discount region", so price can react from a discount key level and expand straight
  to the DOL without rebalancing the range. *(10:26–13:07)*
- **Bearish daily OHLC**: on a bearish day, the daily candle first forms its high at a
  4H key level above a 4H candle range, then expands lower. *(14:08, 15:44–16:00)*

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] D1 structure must be identified: bearish = rejecting above highs and breaking
  below old lows, with the major expansions to the downside. *(4:36–4:52)*
- [ ] Must identify (1) the next long-term DOL and (2) the current dealing range
  plus its key levels, to decide which profile price is in. *(6:28–6:45)*
- [ ] Default: expect price to rebalance the dealing range (reach premium for bearish)
  before the expansion. *(9:08)*
- [ ] Exception (KOD): if price is very close to the DOL and has formed SMT plus a
  small retracement, the discount key level is enough. Bias for the next day is
  bearish if the previous day reacted from that discount FVG and **closed bearish
  without closing above it**, with "no unfilled liquidity above". *(11:47–14:08)*
- [ ] Once the bias is set, take trades in that direction only. *(14:25)*
- [ ] SMT with a correlated pair (EURUSD): GU took a high that EU failed to take,
  which adds confidence in the bearish bias. *(16:32–16:48)*
- [ ] Inside the killzone or KOD sequence, a full H4 dealing range is not needed. A key
  level (here a 4H order block) plus a candle range is enough. *(17:04)* (Note: the
  caption reads "kill zone or the order kill zone sequence", probably "KOD sequence".)
- [ ] During London open: wait for price to take the 4H candle's high and test the
  4H OB key level. Only then drop to M15. *(17:21–17:51)*
- [ ] M15 entry confirmation = an order block forms (here a bearish engulfing M15
  candle that closes lower). *(18:39–18:56)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: at the open of the M15 order block (bearish), straight after it forms. *(18:56)*
- Stop: above the order block. *(18:56)*
- Target: 3R planned. He closed manually just before the final leg. *(18:56–19:12)*
- Timeframe pairs used: D1 bias → H4 context/CRT → M15 entry. *(0:48, 1:39, 2:44)*
- Time-of-day / session filters: London open session. The Asian range high lined up
  with the 4H CRT high and was the liquidity taken. *(15:13, 18:23)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- "Extremely close to the HTF DOL" (KOD trigger) is not quantified. Proposal: distance
  from price to DOL < X% (e.g. 25%) of the dealing-range height, **and** an SMT plus a
  retracement that stays below the dealing-range equilibrium.
- "No unfilled liquidity above": proposal: no untested FVG/OB between the current
  price and the dealing-range high.
- Which 4H candle is the candle range: not specified here (see video 05/06).
- Exiting before the target was discretionary.

## Conflicts with other sources
- The KOD exception overrides the "always rebalance the dealing range first" rule
  used in video 03. Video 06 (20:36–21:24) states the same exception for the bullish
  side (it ends in the premium region).
