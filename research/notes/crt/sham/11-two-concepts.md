## Summary
Sham says every strategy has to answer two questions: which direction to trade
(**daily bias**) and when that direction is valid enough to execute (**entry
confirmation**). He shows a simplified version of his own three-step plan.
(1) Daily bias from market structure plus candle closure. (2) Find a 4H candle
range from a daily or 4H dealing range and a key level. (3) Confirm the entry on
the 15m with an order block that forms at a key time. The rest of the video
shows P&L screenshots from June to August as promo. (Captions write "buys" for
"bias".)

## Definitions
- **Daily bias**: whether to look for longs, shorts, or stay neutral today. Set
  from (a) market structure and (b) candle closure. *(0:31, 1:34)*
- **Market structure**: bullish means price rejects below lows and breaks above
  highs. Cycle: expansion → retracement → once the retracement completes, target
  the opposing swing point (in a bullish structure, the next swing high is the
  draw on liquidity). *(1:51–2:55)*
- **Close inside**: a candle trades past a level, range or previous candle's
  high or low and then closes back inside it. This is rejection and points to a
  retracement or reversal toward the opposite side. *(3:27–3:59)*
- **Close outside**: a candle trades past the previous candle's high or low and
  closes beyond it. This is continuation in that direction. *(4:31–5:04)*
- **Retracement draw**: the minimum target is the opposite extreme of the
  rejection candle. If the retracement continues, the targets are (1) the low of
  the candle that started the retracement and (2) the EQ (50%) of the daily
  dealing range. *(6:25, 7:13)*
- **Entry confirmation**: a 15m order block (a change in the state of delivery)
  that formed **inside a key time**. *(11:56)*

## Rules / conditions
Daily bias (daily TF), with structure S and candle close C relative to the
previous daily candle:
- [ ] S bullish, C trades above the previous high and closes inside → expect a
  retracement (bearish bias for the next day). *(3:59, 6:08)*
- [ ] S bullish, C trades below the previous low and closes inside → retracement
  complete → bullish bias. *(4:15, 8:45–9:17)*
- [ ] S bearish, C trades below the previous low and closes inside →
  retracement (bullish bias). *(4:15)*
- [ ] S bearish, C trades above the previous high and closes inside →
  retracement complete → bearish bias. *(4:31)*
- [ ] C closes beyond the previous candle's extreme → continuation of the current
  leg. That leg is either the expansion or the retracement in progress.
  *(4:47–5:37, 6:57, 9:17)*
- [ ] If the signals conflict (e.g. retracement has reached EQ, so structure says
  done, but C closed outside, so continuation): **neutral**, no trade the next
  day. *(7:44–8:30)*

Candle range (4H)
- [ ] The 4H dealing range must be in the bias direction (bullish bias → bullish
  dealing range). *(10:20)*
- [ ] A key level (e.g. a FVG) must sit in the discount region of the range (for
  longs) or premium (for shorts). *(11:24)*
- [ ] Stay risk-off until price rebalances the dealing range and tests the key
  level. *(12:29)*
- [ ] The CRT is the 4H candle that **closed inside the discount key level**
  ("makes the most sense" per his protocols). *(12:45–13:00)*

Entry confirmation (15m)
- [ ] Wait for the 15m to purge the 4H CRT's low (for longs). *(13:00–13:15)*
- [ ] Then wait for a 15m order block / CSD in the trade direction. *(13:15–13:31)*
- [ ] The order block must have formed at a key time (e.g. the 3:00 a.m. or
  9:00 a.m. macro). Otherwise the idea is **invalidated**. *(11:56–12:14, 13:48)*

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: limit order at the high of the bullish 15m order block (the low of the
  bearish one). *(13:31)*
- Stop: below the order block (above it for shorts). *(13:31)*
- Target: not stated. The bias target is the next swing high or low. *(9:01)*
- Timeframe pairs used: daily (bias) → 4H (dealing range, key level, CRT) → 15m
  (purge, OB, timing).
- Time-of-day / session filters: OB must form in a key time. Examples: the 3:00
  a.m. and 9:00 a.m. macros (assumed NY time). *(13:48)*

## Vague / discretionary parts
- **"Makes the most sense as a candle range"** (13:00): proposed rule: the first
  4H candle that closes inside the key-level zone after the dealing range is
  rebalanced.
- **Retracement completed**: proposed rule: price reaches ≤ EQ (bullish) of the
  current daily dealing range, **and** a daily close back inside after trading
  below the previous low.
- **Market structure label**: needs a swing algorithm (e.g. a fractal of N=2 or 3
  plus close-based breaks).
- **Key time list**: only 3 a.m. and 9 a.m. are named. Proposed: ICT macros
  (xx:50–xx:10) during the London and NY killzones; test variants.
- **Order block**: proposed rule: the last opposite candle (or run) before a 15m
  close beyond the previous swing (CSD).
