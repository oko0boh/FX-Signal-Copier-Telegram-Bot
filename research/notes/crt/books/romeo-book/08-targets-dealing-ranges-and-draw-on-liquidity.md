## Summary
Target 1 is the 50% level of the range and target 2 is the opposite end,
with a partial always taken at 50%. Direction comes from the draw on liquidity,
which is the most important step. A dealing range splits an expansion swing
into premium and discount, and tells you which half to trade from.

## Definitions
- **Dealing range** [S, clear], p.96: when price takes out both a swing low and
  a swing high and then makes an expansion swing, that expansion swing is the
  dealing range. Lower half = discount, upper half = premium. Same idea as a PD
  array matrix, but for expansion swings. The leg need not contain an FVG; the
  slide does not mention one.
- **Draw on liquidity (DOL)** [S, clear], p.229–230, 233: the place price is
  most likely heading. Short-term DOL ends retracements or the first move;
  long-term DOL is the larger expansion target. Forms: range high/low,
  candle-range high/low, HTF key levels, premium/discount and equilibrium of
  a dealing range, expansion swings (p.234).
- **OHLC / OLHC** [R and S, clear], p.1–2, 41, 156–158: a candle is read as
  open (range, generating liquidity), then a manipulation to the low or high
  (purging liquidity), then expansion to the other side (neutralising
  liquidity), then close (reset). Bullish candle = OLHC; bearish = OHLC. The
  manipulation extreme "always" tests an HTF key level (p.158).

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Take 50% off at the 50% of any range or move (p.19 handwritten, 36).
  Rulebook CRT 50% = candle midpoint; the slides say the 50% of the range.
- [ ] Get out or take a partial as soon as an opposing key level is reached
  (p.162).
- [ ] Bullish draw: trade only from discount; premium key level + expansion =
  retracement, discount key level + expansion = reversal. Bearish draw: mirror
  (p.98–100).
- [ ] Trade retracements and reversals only after a lower-timeframe
  confirmation (CSD). Avoid trading counter-expansion. Trade only expansion
  toward the DOL (p.101).
- [ ] Dealing ranges are mostly used to build the narrative; they cover at
  least a quarter of market movement (p.103–104).
- [ ] Long-term DOL = expansions and reversals; short-term DOL = retracements
  (p.238).
- [ ] IF-THEN example (p.237): IF order flow is bullish, we rejected a low,
  the profile is a retracement and a candle range is present, THEN first draw
  is the candle-range high, next the opposing swing high, then the expansion.
- [ ] Targets seen in the Romeo handwriting: the old high/low or "magnet
  zone" (p.5, 9, 11); the previous low of a month/week/day (p.11).

## Entry, stop, target
- Target 1: 50% of the range (p.21, 36, 152, 249, p.9 handwritten).
- Target 2: opposite end of the range, or key level/gravity point (p.9, 21, 36).
