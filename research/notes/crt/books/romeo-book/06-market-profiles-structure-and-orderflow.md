## Summary
The market is either in a trend or a range. In a bullish trend highs are
broken and lows are rejected; in a bearish trend lows are broken and highs are
rejected. "Market profile" in this book means which of five phases price is in
now, and a table maps the last profile plus the presence of a key level to the
next outcome.

## Definitions
- **Bullish / bearish order flow** [R/S, clear], p.45, 198, 255: bullish =
  rejects below lows and breaks above highs; bearish = the opposite. A market
  where both highs and lows are rejected is a range (p.198).
- **Market shift** [R, clear], p.45–46: needs a close beyond the important
  high (or low). When bullish, wait for the high that broke a low to be broken;
  confirmation is one close above; preferably displacement, then consolidation,
  then a retracement into the high, then a second impulse leg. Bearish is the
  mirror.
- **Five profiles** [S, clear], p.254: consolidation/range, manipulation,
  expansion, retracement, reversal. A simpler pair, expansion and range, is
  shown first (p.75).
- **Short/intermediate swings** [S, clear], p.137: STL/STH and ITL/ITH are used
  for invalidation. No bar count is given.
- **Structural shift** [S, clear], p.138–139: bearish shift = a low that broke
  a high and led price to a key level is itself broken; bullish is the mirror.
  Needs HTF draw in sync, an HTF key level, and a key time/macro.
- **IF-THEN principle** [R via Room, clear], p.38, 200: when the HTF is bullish
  the LTF breaks the highs and rejects the lows; when bearish, highs are
  rejected and lows broken.

## Profile + key level table (S, p.87, clear)
| Last profile | Key level there? | Outcome | Trade? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expansion | no | retracement / range | avoid |
| Expansion | yes | reversal | trade |
| Ranging | no | manipulation | avoid |
| Ranging | yes | expansion | trade |
| Reversal | no | retracement | avoid |
| Reversal | yes | range | avoid |
| Retracement | yes | expansion | trade |
| Manipulation | – | expansion | trade |
Only expansion and reversal outcomes are traded; avoid ranging, manipulation
and retracement outcomes (p.87).

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Use the advanced structure only on higher timeframes: intraday traders
  use only the 4H, swing traders only the daily (p.141).
- [ ] Structure is a confluence, never the main basis for a trade (p.134,
  141); in the R handwritten notes bias comes from market structure first (p.4).
- [ ] Higher timeframe finds key levels; the lower timeframe profiles. Pairs:
  daily KL / H1 profile; H4 KL / M15; H1 KL / M5; M15 KL / M1 (p.87).
- [ ] Stick to the HTF bias until it is invalidated (p.4); a timeframe is
  bullish only until it reaches an HTF key level (p.61).
- [ ] False market shifts give the fastest moves (p.47, handwritten, partly).
- [ ] Do not trade a counter-trend: if the HTF is bearish, highs get rejected;
  Q&A example of a trader who shorted an old high and was run over by an HTF
  bullish flow (p.67–68, handwritten, clear).
