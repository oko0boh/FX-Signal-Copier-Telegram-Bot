## Summary
Model #1 is the UTS entry model: a thick candle runs into an old high or low,
and you enter when a later candle closes beyond it. "A Quick Buck" trades the
candle after the one that hit the key level. The three "bread and butter" models
are the 3-candle model, Model #1 and "A Quick Buck" (p.33).

## Definitions
- **Model #1 steps** [R, clear], p.27 (typed UTS slide):
  1. Mark the opening price.
  2. Wait for a thick up-close candle into an old high, or a thick down-close
     candle into an old low.
  3. Wait for price to close above the down-close candle, or below the
     up-close candle.
  4. Enter at that specific candle's high or low.
  5. Trade in the direction of the higher-timeframe order flow.
- **Entry trigger** [R, clear], p.28: a close beyond the candle that
  liquidated the high or low. No trigger, no entry.
- **Pattern shape** [R, clear], p.28: an order block forms stabbing into the
  previous candle's high, or a range forms with the order block stabbing into
  the current candle's range high/low. Sequence: open, range, manipulation,
  expansion (PO3).
- **"A Quick Buck"** [R, clear], p.29, 31: trade the candle after the one that
  hits the key level. Bullish key level: buy below the open. Bearish: sell above
  the open.
- **Trading model guide** [R, clear], p.33: a model needs an entry price zone,
  an entry time zone, an exit level and a stop level; it must repeat daily and
  be readable by two people in the same terms.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Entry only in the direction of the HTF order flow (p.27).
- [ ] The turtle soup must be at a key level and at a specific time (p.40).
- [ ] If Model #1 fails, there are times that make it work and times that do
  not (p.27 handwritten, partly): time is part of the rule.
- [ ] Entry in a turtle soup is the close above or below it (p.27 handwritten).
- [ ] Do not sell below a low or buy above a high without a turtle soup
  (p.199, [Room]).

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: the close beyond the model candle; price taken at that candle's
  high/low (p.27). Whether "high/low" means the wick extreme or the body edge
  is **not written**; the slide says "the specific candle's high or low".
  [clear text, partly the interpretation]
- Stop: the swing high (bearish) or low (bullish) that formed into the key
  level (p.27 handwritten, clear); or beyond week 2 / Tuesday / London high or
  low for a weekly model (p.7).
- Stops that are too tight get hit before the target: the Q&A says widen the
  stop and use better entry points (p.69, handwritten, clear).
- Target: first 50% of the range, then the opposite end (see file 04).
- Order block entry (candle 2) and kiss of death entry (candle 3): see file 04.
- Timeframes seen: entries on 5m and 15m (p.28 handwritten "5 min and 15 min",
  p.18 "best time to trade daily range 5 min and 15 min"). [partly]

## Conflicts with other sources
- C-07: the slide supports "close beyond the model candle, enter at its high or
  low". It does not choose wick or body. The rulebook default (wick high) stays
  a guess.
- C-19: Romeo handwriting p.25 prefers order blocks that had a failed order
  block before them, and p.114 (S) says ignore colours and patterns, trade
  logic. Neither forbids order blocks.
- C-20: stop = the swing that formed into the key level (p.27). Matches the
  rulebook's "turtle soup / KoD extreme" more than "model candle extreme".
