## Summary
The PDF has 268 pages and no text layer. It is a scanned notebook: course slides
and chart screenshots with a student's handwriting on top, not a book with
chapters or a table of contents. Text was read with OCR, then handwritten and
unclear pages were checked by eye (pages 4, 5, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, 18, 28, 69,
118, 148). Where OCR and the page image disagreed, the image won.

About 60% of the pages come from a different teacher (Sham, "CRT Unlocked"),
not from Romeo. Every statement in these notes carries a source tag so the
rulebook's priority order (Romeo, then Sham) can be applied.

## Source tags used in all notes
- **[R]** Romeo-family material: handwritten notes (p.1–19), "UTS" slides
  (p.20–50, logo on p.28, "Q2 2024"), "Secrets of Time" slides (p.51–70,
  labelled Q1 2024 on p.54), Romeo tweet and notes (p.266–267).
- **[S]** Sham's course "CRT Unlocked" (p.71–197 and p.225–264). Pages say so:
  p.181 ("ECR invented by Sham"), p.248 ("CRT was authored by Romeo and coined
  by Sham"), p.225 ("Sham's continuation").
- **[Room]** "TS Room / Soup Room" chapter notes (p.198–224). Author not named.
  It credits the IF-THEN idea to Romeo (p.200) and repeats Secrets-of-Time
  content (Wednesday model, 4H blocks).
- Confidence: **clear** = typed text or clearly written; **partly** = OCR plus
  image still leaves doubt; **guess** = my reading of a diagram or bad
  handwriting.

## Things to know before using these notes
- Pages carry watermarks of Telegram channels that redistribute paid courses
  (t.me/paidstuff4free, @CrtUnlockedClone, @cpileaked, Traders_Hive). The PDF
  stays local (git-ignored). These notes are in our own words with no long
  quotes.
- The "UTS" slides name no author on the pages read. I call them Romeo-family
  because the handwritten notes and the rulebook's earlier sources line up with
  them. This is an assumption, not proof.
- Handwriting on p.1–2 is mostly unreadable by OCR; those pages are summarised
  only where the image was read.
