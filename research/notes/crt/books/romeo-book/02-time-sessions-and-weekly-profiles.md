## Summary
The notes put time ahead of price. Every candle is split into three stages
(accumulation, manipulation, distribution). The best turtle soups form at the
open of a new candle, at fixed hours of the New York day, and at fixed days of
the week. The weekly profiles are three named patterns, not six.

## Definitions
- **Time-based model** [R, clear]: pick a time window first, then read price
  only inside it (p.52, 210). Master one candle of one timeframe.
- **PO3 / AMD per candle** [R, clear]: accumulation (range), manipulation
  (judas / turtle soup), distribution (expansion). Daily: three 8-hour parts
  (p.7 handwritten). A candle may also have a fourth part, retracement (p.2).
- **4H blocks** [R, clear]: the day is six 4H blocks starting 01:00, 05:00,
  09:00, 13:00, 17:00, 21:00 NY ("1-5-9") (p.14, 53, 211). Also splits of 24h
  by 3, 4 or 2 give 8h, 6h, 12h blocks (p.14).
- **90-minute cycle** [R, clear]: 01:00–01:30 accumulation, 01:30–02:00
  manipulation, 02:00–02:30 distribution; same shape from 05:00 (p.213).
- **Manipulation length** [R, clear]: about 15–20 minutes inside an hour
  (60/4, 60/3) (p.15 handwritten).
- **Sessions (EST)** [S, clear], p.228: Asian 20:00–00:00; London open
  02:00–05:00; London lunch 05:00–07:00; New York open 07:00–10:00; London close
  10:00–12:00. Indices: NY AM 08:30–11:00, NY PM 13:30–16:00. Refined key
  times: 03–04, 06–07, 08:30–09:30, 10–11.
- **Time ranges** [S, clear], p.148: CBDR drawn 16:00–20:00, Asian range
  20:00–00:00, entry window from 02:00 (chart labels).
- **Open prices** [R/Room, clear]: mark the 00:00 NY open before it arrives
  (p.56, 215); weekly open is "Sunday open at 17:00" (p.54, 214).

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Time before price: price levels mean little until time is right, and
  time means little unless price is at a key level (p.4 handwritten, p.15).
- [ ] Bullish: look for the turtle soup below the candle's open. Bearish:
  above the open (p.41, 43, 200).
- [ ] High-probability turtle soups form at the **open of new candles**, not
  at the end of old candles, and accelerate fast from the current price (p.40,
  41, 208).
- [ ] Intraday high-probability times: 03:00–05:00 and 09:00–11:00 NY
  (p.28, UTS slide; "NY time" written beside it). Purge key times: 01, 03, 06,
  08, 09, 11 (p.258, [S]).
- [ ] Forex uses 01:00 as its key manipulation hour, indices use 09:00
  (p.15 handwritten).
- [ ] Kill zone profiles [S] (p.229): Asian = range; London open = manipulation
  that often makes the high/low of day; London lunch = reversal, retracement or
  continuation; NY open and London close = turtle soup plus key level.
- [ ] Weekly: low of the week forms Mon–Wed, opposite end Thu/Fri (p.53,
  handwritten, partly). Wednesday almost never makes both the high and low of
  the week (p.212, [Room]).
- [ ] Monthly [S] (p.228): week 1 range, week 2 manipulation or expansion,
  week 3 distribution, week 4 continuation, reversal or reset. UTS slide says
  the month's high/low normally forms in weeks 2 and 3 (p.35).
- [ ] A data standard: back-test at least 1 year (5–10 ideal), forward-test 3–6
  months before trusting a time model (p.54, 212).

## Weekly profiles [S] (p.116–123, mostly diagrams)
Three named profiles, each drawn with a bullish and a bearish version and a
key level at both ends:
1. **Classic expansion week** (versions 1 and 2). Monday range, Tuesday
   manipulation, Wednesday expansion, Thursday range or reversal, Friday
   reversal (p.118, handwritten, clear).
2. **Midweek reversal** (p.120).
3. **Consolidation reversal** (p.120–121).
They only work together with key levels and a PD array or dealing range
(p.116). Monday's price action is the framework, it can change, and the news
calendar should be used (p.117).

## Intraday profiles [S] (p.123–130, diagrams)
Normal protraction, delayed protraction, NY continuation, NY reversal (two
versions). Drawn against London open, NY open and London close with a key
level above and below. The pages hold no written rules, so the exact times of
each phase are **not covered** [partly: the diagrams were read from OCR text
only].

## Wednesday model [R slides p.54–58, Room p.214–216]
Monday range, Tuesday sweeps and closes back, Wednesday prints the classic
buy/sell candle with the weekly direction. Steps: HTF bias and narrative;
mark the 00:00 open and the previous candle's range; mark a key level for the
high/low of the day; use CBDR/Asian range standard deviations to estimate where
the low/high forms; wait for a pattern **below the open** after the Judas swing;
write down what you see (p.56, 215). Buy below Sunday's open Mon–Wed and exit
by Thursday (p.214). In the daily version: 01:00 candle makes the high/low,
price returns to the open, then the 09:00 candle gives the retracement and
second leg (p.215–216).

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry: after the Judas swing, back through the open (p.215).
- Stop: week 2 high/low, Tuesday high/low, London high/low (p.7 handwritten, clear).
- Target: hold to the IPDA range/magnet zone; take 50% at the candle midpoint
  (p.7).

## Vague / discretionary parts
- "Time deviation" (4, 3, 2 parts of a candle) is never given as a measurable
  rule beyond the 8h/6h/12h and 90-minute splits.
- Weekday probabilities (Tuesday 70%, 81% between Sunday open and Tuesday
  London) are claims without a sample. Test, do not adopt.

## Conflicts with other sources
- C-02: this book puts the weekly open at **Sunday 17:00** (p.54, 214) and the
  daily open at 00:00 NY. It does not state a Friday close.
- G-23: the rulebook's "sweep at the end of the previous candle" (romeo/09)
  is the opposite of p.40/41/208 ("at the open of new candles").
- Rulebook 6.2 rejects "10–25 setups a week". p.53 and 211 claim exactly
  that (10 high-probability, 25 good quality). Same source family as before.
