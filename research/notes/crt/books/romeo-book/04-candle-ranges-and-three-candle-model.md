## Summary
Every candle is a range. A range is either turtle-souped (false break) or
broken through and retested. The three-candle model is: candle 1 is the range,
candle 2 manipulates it, candle 3 distributes. Targets are the 50% level then
the opposite end of candle 1. Sham's final chapter says only the third candle
should be traded by most students.

## Definitions
- **Range trading rules** [R, clear], p.36: buy low, sell high; buy range-low
  rejections, sell range-high rejections; do not buy the range high or sell the
  range low; never enter in the middle; the extremes are the playground and
  the rest is a no-trade zone. Any first breakout of a range is a fake move
  (p.36 handwritten).
- **Candles are ranges** [R, clear], p.10, 37: a close beyond a candle means
  it is truly broken; a wick beyond it that closes back is a turtle soup.
- **CRT range = the whole candle** [S, clear], p.249: the entire price action
  of candle 1 (high to low, wicks included) is the range, even if it was not a
  consolidation.
- **Candle 2** [S, clear], p.249: manipulates candle 1, usually a turtle soup.
  Closes back inside = higher odds of a move to the other end; closes outside =
  lower odds, range still stands, caution.
- **Candle 3** [S, clear], p.200, 249: the distribution candle; fastest, quick
  and heavy, the easiest entries.
- **CRT types** [S, clear], p.248: 3-candle CRT (best, most reliable) and
  "inside CRT" (one candle is the range, then several candles manipulate and
  distribute).
- **Monday range model** [R, clear], p.21–23: apply the range rules to Monday;
  Tuesday is the turtle soup, Wednesday the continuation. Monday is either
  turtle-souped or broken through and used as a key level.
- **Asian range model** [R, clear], p.24: Asian range is 20:00–00:00 NY.
  Target the 50% and the opposite end.
- **Three-candle formation** [R handwritten, clear], p.13: if Monday is an
  expansion do nothing; if Monday or Tuesday closes beyond the range, go with
  the order flow; Monday range, Tuesday manipulation, Wednesday expansion.

## Rules / conditions
- [ ] Candle 3 only for most traders ("confirmational CRT": trade after the
  confirmation, forget candles 1 and 2) (p.262).
- [ ] "Advanced CRT" trades candle 2 or 3 before confirmation; beginners and
  intermediates should skip it (p.263).
- [ ] Confirmations for a CRT trade, minimum three (p.201, [Room]): a timed
  CRT, a timed turtle soup (purge at a key time), an HTF key level; extras are
  inside bars and a lower-timeframe CRT inside the higher-timeframe CRT.
- [ ] Find CRT at range highs/lows, rejections of highs/lows, HTF key levels,
  major purges, the manipulation phase, and key times/macros (p.259).
- [ ] Both formation and purge must be at key times: formed at fixed days and
  hours, purged at 01, 03, 06, 08, 09 or 11 (p.258).
- [ ] CRT is never a standalone pattern; it needs the logic around it
  (p.248, 253).
- [ ] Do not trade right after the open; wait for the high/low of the candle
  at an HTF key level; take a partial or exit when an opposing key level is
  reached (p.162).
- [ ] Close above a candle = higher prices; close below = lower; wick below =
  higher; wick above = lower (p.38, 62).

## Entry, stop, target
- Entry on candle 2: order block entry. After candle 2 purges the range and
  displaces the other way, enter on the return to the order block (p.250).
- Entry on candle 3: "kiss of death" entry, the last turtle soup before price
  reaches the opposing draw on liquidity or the opposite extreme (p.250–252).
- Stop: see `05-model-1-and-entry-models.md`.
- Target 1: 50% of the range. Target 2: opposite end (p.21, 36, 152, 249). Always
  take a partial at 50% (p.36, 19).
- Timeframe pairs: see `07-timeframe-alignment.md`.
- Session filter: 5am 4H CRT, then a 15m CRT at its high/low, purge during the
  9am hour (accepted until 10:30), enter when the 2nd 15m candle closes or on a
  15m/5m order block (p.201, [Room]).

## Conflicts with other sources
- C-04: this book (S p.249) does not require a close back inside; it only
  raises the odds. Romeo handwritten (p.10) says a close beyond = real break,
  wick only = turtle soup. Both keep "close back inside" as the preferred
  signal.
- C-09: matches Romeo (candle 3 for most, candle 2 advanced).
- C-18: p.200 says candle 3 is the fastest. No slow-grind case is shown.
