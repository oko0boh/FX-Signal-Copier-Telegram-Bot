## Summary
Only what the pages say. "Not covered" means I read all 268 pages and found
nothing. Tags: **[R]** Romeo-family, **[S]** Sham's CRT Unlocked, **[Room]**
unattributed Soup Room notes (see `01-provenance-and-page-map.md`). Confidence:
clear / partly / guess. Under the rulebook's priority order, [S] answers rank
below Romeo's own videos.

**Biggest findings**
1. This is not a Romeo book. It is a scanned notebook; about 60% of it is
   Sham's course.
2. The CRT range is the **whole candle including wicks** (p.249) and the 50%
   target is measured on it (answers Q7).
3. The **key-level timeframe table** (p.205) matches option (c) in C-01, and
   the book has no pair table like (a) or (b).
4. Weekly open is **Sunday 17:00** in this book (p.54, 214); no Friday close
   is given (C-02).
5. Turtle soups happen at the **open of new candles**, not the end of old
   ones (p.40, 208), which is the reverse of the proxy in G-23.
6. G-03 "six possibilities", G-05 "closing-hour logic", the key-level
   **choice algorithm** (G-01 core) and the OTE zone numbers are **not covered**.

  book's three profiles are in file 02. ICT's CBDR is drawn here as 16:00–20:00,
