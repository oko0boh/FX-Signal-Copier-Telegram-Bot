## 1. Summary

1. A beginner-level retelling of Romeo's 2025 "CRT secrets" episodes 1-9 (romeo/03, 05-12); episode 10 ("clean close") and all CRTology 2026 material (romeo/16-18) are **not** covered.
2. Most pages are motivation, homework and checklists; the rule content is thin and almost all of it is already in the rulebook.
3. Its real value: (a) it **dates several of Romeo's chart examples** (EURUSD Jul/Aug/Sep 2025, BTC Aug-Oct 2025), (b) model #1 and KoD diagrams that show the trigger reference, (c) a 5-step candle-3 procedure, a 4-step top-down recipe and a 7-item checklist.
4. It does **not** contain: the weekly "six possibilities", a timeframe pairing table, OTE numbers, weekly open/close times, or how to choose the correct key level.
5. Several statements contradict Romeo's own videos or the book's own charts (section 4); treat it as low-confidence corroboration, never as a tie-breaker against Romeo.

## 2. Adds to the rulebook

Each item: what the book says (own words), page, rulebook ID affected, and how much weight it deserves.

### 2.1 Model #1 (DEF-08, CRT-R39, CRT-R49, C-07)

- **A1. Six-step recipe, both sides** (p.2-4, PDF 10-12). Bearish: old high → price stabs into it → a thick up-close candle → enter when price **closes below that specific candle** → stop above that candle's high → target "the next lows below". Bullish is the mirror. Matches DEF-08/CRT-R39/R49. New only in the target: **"next lows/highs"** = the swings-one-by-one target of CRT-R54 (not the CRT 50%) for a stand-alone model #1. Confidence: medium (it repeats romeo/03 10:34).
- **A2. Trigger reference = the candle's wick extreme (diagram evidence)** (Fig 1.2 p.3 / PDF 11; Fig 1.3 p.4 / PDF 12). In the bearish drawing the "Model #1" line is drawn at the **low of the whole up-close sweep candle**, and the entry candle is the first one whose body closes below that line; candles that only wick below it are not the entry. In the bullish drawing the line sits at the **wick high** of the thick down-close candle. Supports the C-07 default `model1_trigger_ref = wick`. TbV drawing, not stated in words; confidence low-medium.
- **A3. The model candle is not always the bar with the extreme** (Fig 1.3, PDF 12, TbV). In the bullish drawing the boxed "thick candle" setup includes a later small up-close bar that prints a slightly lower low than the thick down-close candle; the model line is still taken from the thick down-close candle. This argues against the PROPOSED tie-break in DEF-08 ("M = the bar with the lowest low"); a better proxy is "the thickest opposite-close bar among the sweep bars", with the stop under the lowest low of the group. Confidence: low (one drawing).
- **A4. FVG confluence** (p.5, Fig 1.4 PDF 13): model #1 "with an FVG" is stronger. Already CRT-R46. The chart shows the FVG directly above the bearish model #1 candle (EURUSD, test case T3).
- **A5. Partial model #1 on the weekly** (p.66, PDF 74): a weekly close below the previous up-close candles "could be model #1 — but check criteria"; if price then pumps back toward that candle, expect a retrace. Already DEF-08 "partial model #1" (romeo/12 4:41).

### 2.2 Kiss of death in real time (DEF-13 KoD-R, G-15, CRT-R48, C-11)

- **A6. KoD entry recipe** (p.11, PDF 19). TbV turns KoD-R into a mechanical entry: (1) mark the CRT range and target, (2) wait for a turtle soup of a level, (3) find the **KoD candle = a big candle against the trade** (a big up-close candle in a bearish setup, p.9), (4) enter when price **closes beyond the KoD candle**, (5) stop beyond the turtle-soup point, (6) target the CRT objective. In other words: **KoD entry = a model #1 formed at the final turtle soup inside an active CRT.** This is the clearest real-time rule for G-15 we have. Confidence: medium as an interpretation (romeo/05 18:27 also enters the KoD with "model #1"), low as a definition of *which* TS is the final one.
- **A7. Where the KoD sits** (TbV, two charts): Fig 2.4 (p.11, PDF 19, ETH daily) puts the KoD **at the start of candle 3**, as a sweep of a level built inside candle 2, with entry right after; Fig 2.3 right (p.10, PDF 18, EURUSD) puts it **after the 50% (TP1) was reached**, as a small rally/TS just above 50% before the drop to the CRT low. The book also says the KoD "happens" in candle 3 (p.8). So for G-15: the KoD window = any time after C2 has closed and before TP2, not only after TP1. Current proxy (`kod_r_requires_tp1: true`) is too narrow; test `false` as well.
- **A8. KoD + FVG and KoD + SMT** as stronger signals (p.12-13, p.43): confluence items for CRT-R46. Fig 2.6 (PDF 21) shows a bearish KoD wick into a bearish FVG.

### 2.3 Candle 3 procedure, stops and targets (CRT-R30, CRT-R44, CRT-R50, CRT-R53, C-05, C-09)

- **A9. Five-step candle-3 entry** (p.49, PDF 57): (1) wait for C2 to finish completely, (2) look for a turtle soup / liquidity grab, (3) enter when C3 "confirms", (4) stop **beyond the manipulation** (i.e. beyond C2's sweep extreme), (5) take profit at the **50%, then C1's high/low**. Confirms CRT-R30 and CRT-R53; step 4 gives a stop anchor for C3 entries that the rulebook only implies (CRT-R50 "TS extreme"): `stop = C2.high` (bear) / `C2.low` (bull). Confidence: medium.
- **A10. Target labels** (Fig 2.3, p.10, PDF 18): "50% – Target 1", "CRTL – Target 2" and "CRTH – Ts". Same as CRT-R53.
- **A11. Weak turtle soup → previous day's high** (p.67, PDF 75): restates romeo/12 (G-24). No threshold.
- **A12. 50% = "first mission"** (p.57, PDF 65): reaching 50% counts as a success; same as CRT-R59.

### 2.4 Key levels (DEF-14, CRT-R18-R20, G-01)

- **A13. Candidate list** (p.30, PDF 38): old highs, old lows, and "places where big traders left footprints (SMT)". The SMT item is new for DEF-14 in Romeo mode: a level where an SMT formed can serve as a key level (Sham already uses an SMT purge for OBs, sham/02 8:42). TbV wording; confidence low.
- **A14. Trade TO or FROM a key level, but pick one per level** (p.33, PDF 41). "To": trade the move into the level (sell down into a bullish level). "From": trade the bounce after it. TbV calls choosing one a "golden rule". This maps to two setup types for romeo/08's V-shape, but Romeo trades both (section 4, X7). Proposed use: a parameter `P:kl_trade_side = from | to | both` (default `from`, the only one the rulebook codes).
- **A15. Fake bounce first** (p.32-33, p.35): the market makes a fake bounce before the real one; wait for it. Same as CRT-R20 / DEF-11.
- **A16. Level hygiene** (p.35, PDF 43): mark only the strongest, clearest levels; check daily/weekly levels first. Supports the G-01 proxy's preference for HTF levels and a small candidate set, but gives **no selection rule**. G-01 remains open.
- **A17. Candle 3 only works at key levels** (p.49, PDF 57); "car = CRT, road = key level, timing = when to drive" (p.34). Same as CRT-R18, plus timing as a third requirement.

### 2.5 Market profile and true MSS (DEF-25, DEF-10, CRT-R07, G-04)

- **A18. TbV's operational meaning of "market profile"** (p.66 PDF 74; p.69 PDF 77; appendix p.73 PDF 81): step 2 of the recipe is "mark highs and lows on the relevant TF and note any true MSS", and the checklist reduces the market-profile check to **"has there been a true structure shift?"**. So in TbV's reading, `market_profile_ok(dir) := a true MSS (DEF-10) in dir has formed on the relevant TF`. This is a computable proxy for CRT-R07 and an alternative to the G-04 swing-sequence proxy. It is TbV's reading of ep.9, not Romeo's definition (Romeo calls market profile "one of the most crucial elements" but never defines it, romeo/16 23:33). Confidence: low-medium.
- **A19. True MSS wording** (p.66, PDF 74): "a clean close above/below a prior structure point". Close-based, as DEF-10. See X2 for the looser version on p.67.

### 2.6 Timeframes (CRT-R01-R03, C-01, C-10)

- **A20. Tradable candles for beginners: 4H, daily, weekly only** (p.25, PDF 33; p.27). Supports the CRT-R02 default `[4H, D, W]`.
- **A21. "Think in candles" hierarchy** (p.49-50, PDF 57-58): weekly = big picture, daily = main setup, 4H = entry timing, 1H = fine-tuning. This is a one-step-down chain W → D → 4H → 1H, not a pairing table. It does not match any of C-01's tables exactly (a: W→4H, D→1H; b: W→1H, D→15m). Fig 7.2 (PDF 58) shows D/H4/H1/M15 panels but is a generic illustration with wrong labels ("3-hour chart (H1)", "D2"); ignore it. The worked trade (p.67) uses a **daily candle with a 5-minute entry**, same as romeo/12 (C-01 option d). Confidence: low; no new pairing rule.
- **A22. Two-timeframe analysis** (p.16, p.19): HTF = CRT structure, support/resistance, narrative; LTF = trigger, stop, timing. Same as CRT-R01.

### 2.7 Weekly candle, time and days (DEF-31, CRT-R14, CRT-R35, CRT-R56, C-02, G-22)

- **A23. Weekly open** (p.43, PDF 51): "Monday's opening sets the stage". Weak support for the Monday 00:00 weekly open (C-02). No clock time, no close time.
- **A24. Weekly rhythm** (p.44, PDF 52, TbV): Monday = open and frequent manipulation; Tuesday-Wednesday = the real move develops; Thursday-Friday = distribution and preparation for next week. Stated as repeating every week. This differs from Romeo's templates (X4).
- **A25. Session for the entry** (p.67, PDF 75): Tuesday, **London session**, to "catch part of one daily candle". Same as romeo/12 15:28. The checklist (p.69, p.73) makes "time/session fits the plan" a required item and adds "right time of day/week". For G-22 this supports using the London window as the Romeo-mode intraday filter (`P:romeo_use_killzones` with London 02:00-05:00 NY as the first variant), but no hours are given.
- **A26. Exit by time = session end** (p.68, PDF 76): exits "by price (hit your target)" or "by time (session ends)". Romeo only said both are valid (romeo/12 18:14). TbV's reading gives CRT-R56 a concrete variant: `P:time_exit = end_of_session` (the session in which the trade was opened). Confidence: low.

### 2.8 SMT (DEF-18, CRT-R42, CRT-R57, C-24)

- **A27. SMT only in the HTF direction** (p.68, PDF 76): bullish SMTs only when the HTF is bullish, bearish only when bearish. Same as CRT-R42 / romeo/12.
- **A28. SMT check before every trade** (p.59-60, PDF 67-68): TbV makes "check the correlated market" a non-negotiable pre-trade step (anti-failure checklist item 1). This is a *check*, not a requirement that an SMT be present; it fits the C-24 default (not required for entry, used for invalidation) and implies the bot must always load the paired symbol (`P:smt_pairs`). Pairs named: EURUSD/DXY, Gold/DXY (p.45, new pair for DEF-18; sign not given, presumably inverse), BTC/ETH (p.56).
- **A29. "Every entry is an exit"** (p.43): a reached target is a new entry point. Already in romeo/09 5:02. No rule change.

### 2.9 Structure of a CRT and the journey (DEF-24, C-08, C-09, C-18)

- **A30. Sequence inside the move** (p.18, PDF 26): turtle soup → model #1 → breaker → OTE → kiss of death. Same as romeo/06 (C-08 option 1). The **OTE is named only; no fib zone or rule** is given, so C-06 is untouched.
- **A31. "Every CRT has TS, true MSS, model #1, KoD"** (p.19, PDF 27). TbV overstatement (see X6); useful only as labels for the backtest log (`stage` per CRT-R45).
- **A32. Candle 2 only for advanced traders; beginners trade candle 3 only** (p.15-16, p.48). Same as CRT-R30 / C-09 default.
- **A33. Market cycle** (p.65-66, PDF 73-74): range → turtle soup → trend → range again; near big liquidity pools (especially near ATH with an FVG) expect a reaction. Restates romeo/12.

### 2.10 Risk and journaling (CRT-R62, CRT-R70)

- **A34. Risk 1-2% per trade** (p.17, PDF 25; p.68, PDF 76). TbV number, not Romeo's (CRT-R62 says no source gives one). It gives an upper bound for the user's decision; the rulebook default 0.5% stays below it.
- **A35. Journal formula "Catch – Miss – Avoid – Repeat"** (p.59, PDF 67): taken and won / taken and lost (and why) / not taken (and why) / lessons. Maps cleanly onto the CRT-R70 audit: add `skipped_setups` with a reason, next to `trades_outside_model`.
- **A36. Always write the alternate scenario** (p.64, p.68): where you exit or reduce if wrong. For the bot this is the invalidation set of CRT-R61; no change.
- **A37. Pre-trade checklist** (p.69 PDF 77; p.73 PDF 81): HTF narrative → key level / liquidity pools → market profile (true MSS) → entry model (model #1, KoD or candle 3) → confluence stacking → stop beyond the TS point → time of day/week. Good as the order of the signal pipeline (CRT-R01, CRT-R37: the entry model is last).
